## Which record controls?

Current official destination rules and issued permit conditions govern an actual trip. The dated country dossiers are the research record. The current structured files under `lib/country-research/` supply the local answer preview. PRODUCT.md and ARCHITECTURE.md preserve the publication and commercial gates. Earlier research is not silently overwritten when a new result supersedes it.

Every future research pass should add a dated snapshot, identify superseded findings, retain exact official links and access dates, and list unresolved contradictions. Keep personal medical and identity records out of these repository documents.
