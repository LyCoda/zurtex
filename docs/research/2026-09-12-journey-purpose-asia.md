# Supplementary journey-purpose evidence

Accessed 2026-09-12. Research notes only; not a complete commercial-import checklist. The linked official pages/PDF sections were opened and read, rather than relying solely on search snippets. These findings supplement the existing country dossiers. Animal-health entry, customs treatment, domestic trading licences, and event permissions are separate questions. None of these notes classifies a movement as commercial merely because it travels as cargo or uses a paid relocation agent. Same-owner relocation is not automatically an ownership transfer; its permitted procedure still depends on the jurisdiction and actual journey.

## HK — Hong Kong

AFCD's import application instructions explicitly require an Animal Trader Licence **in advance when animals are imported for trading purposes**, alongside the Special Permit application. They do not establish a distinct same-owner relocation health pathway. [AFCD Group I application, section I](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/quarantine/qua_ie/qua_ie_ipab/qua_ie_ipab_idc/qua_ie_ipab_idc_Group_I.html).

Separately, domestic trading guidance says dog sales need a licence/permit; consideration can include obligatory adoption/rehoming fees or linked purchases. Free transfers without consideration are not sales; qualifying nonprofit rehoming organisations may seek an exemption. These are **trading controls**, not an exemption from animal-health import requirements. [AFCD trading and breeding guidance, introduction and exemption FAQs](https://www.pets.gov.hk/english/animal_business/animal_trading_and_breeding.html).

Gaps: no exhaustive imported-adoption, breeding-trip or event pathway verified. Obtain AFCD purpose classification for these cases; do not apply a blanket EU-style ownership-transfer definition.

## CN — Mainland China

The official republication of GACC Announcement 5/2019 describes the passenger-carried pet regime: one dog or cat per person per entry, official quarantine/rabies documents, microchip and applicable quarantine. It does **not** establish commercial sale/adoption/breeding eligibility or a separate same-owner relocation category. [Beijing Government / GACC announcement, sections I–III](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/latest/lawsandpolicies/202104/t20210407_2346645.html).

Shanghai's outward-travel instructions direct individual applicants to the Customs **non-commercial pet export** service. This supports the existence of that specific export channel, not a national definition of every ownership-transfer or event shipment. [Shanghai Government export-certificate guide](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Individuals-DailyLife-Pets/20260813/c1956c47aeb3469b98e172b3f49274d3.html).

Gaps: commercial-import admission, cargo handling outside the passenger regime, sale/adoption/breeding/events, and non-Shanghai export procedures remain unverified. Do not infer that passenger entry automatically permits later sale or that cargo automatically means commercial.

## TW — Taiwan

APHIA's FAQ, updated 10 March 2026, explicitly provides a **short-term event application** for dogs/cats entering for international competitions, accompanying participants in international events held in Taiwan, or accompanying government-invited individuals. It requires an application and project-specific quarantine plan at least **60 days before import**; sealed transport to the designated quarantine location follows APHIA instructions. This is an animal-health procedure, not a general exemption for every show or contest. [APHIA FAQ, PDF pages 5 and 7](https://pet-epermit.aphia.gov.tw/files/other/information_146asfiledownload0_14721c.pdf).

The ordinary FAQ uses animal origin/history and return conditions; no separate same-owner relocation rule was established.

Gaps: general sale/adoption/breeding classification and all additional trading permissions were not verified. Mainland-origin special import approval is an additional unresolved issue; the existing ordinary pet checklist must not be presented as sufficient for those cases.

## JP — Japan

AQS requires imported dogs/cats to undergo import quarantine and describes the main health branches by exporting region and preparation. Its main guidance does not establish a separate same-owner relocation health category. [MAFF AQS import/export overview](https://www.maff.go.jp/aqs/english/animal/dog/).

Separately, the Environment Ministry requires registration before operating specified animal businesses. Its **selling** category explicitly includes breeding and importing/exporting for retail or wholesale sale, including intermediaries/agents. Commercial lending for breeding and exhibiting are also listed categories. These are **animal-business/welfare rules**, not evidence of a different AQS vaccine or quarantine schedule. [Environment Ministry, regulated animal businesses](https://www.env.go.jp/nature/dobutsu/aigo/1_law/trader.html).

Gaps: occasional personal purchases, rescue/adoption, nonprofit entities, foreign event participation and complete importer/customs obligations are not fully assessed. Do not claim that this business-registration page proves all ownership transfers use a distinct commercial health regime.

## MY — Peninsular Malaysia

DVS's currently linked non-scheduled-country dog/cat protocol states its purpose coverage is **“Any Purpose”** and requires a MAQIS import permit and government veterinary certification. Purpose alone therefore does not justify inventing a separate universal commercial health schedule. [DVS non-scheduled protocol, section C and sections 1–3](https://www.dvs.gov.my/dvs/resources/user_1/2026/BKPBV/IMPORT%20EKSPORT/%28R2%29-CatsNdogs-NONSCHEDULED_COUNTRIES-revised131213_-_notis.pdf).

There is a material exception: its controlled-dog-breed approval conditions require a personal-pet/not-for-sale declaration; controlled breeds are permitted for the importer's personal pet or security purpose only (section 7.6.1).

Gaps: importer/business licensing, sale/adoption/breeding/event permissions and temporary-entry customs treatment remain unverified. The protocol's updated-country notice conflicts with some older wording; reconcile with DVS/MAQIS. These notes do not cover Sabah or Sarawak or certify unrestricted commercial eligibility.

## TH — Thailand

Thai Customs explicitly publishes **personal pet** procedures for both passengers and the Suvarnabhumi cargo-clearance channel. The cargo process includes airway bills, import permissions and customs declarations. Therefore cargo is not itself evidence of commercial purpose. [Thai Customs personal-pet procedures](https://www.customs.go.th/list_strc_simple_neted.php?ini_content=individual_F01_160913_01&ini_menu=menu_individual_submenu_02&lang=en&left_menu=menu_individual_submenu_02_160421_02&root_left_menu=menu_individual_submenu_02).

The same page offers temporary pet import using ownership evidence and a bond/guarantee of re-export within six months. This is **customs treatment**, not an animal-health exemption or proof that every event qualifies. DLD animal-health permissions remain separate.

Gaps: sale/adoption/commercial breeding classification, trader licensing and event-specific permissions were not established. No separate same-owner relocation health rule verified. Do not confuse personal-pet duties/taxes with commercial-purpose classification.

## PH — Philippines

BAI's 2024 Citizen's Charter defines one-time/pet importers as companion/guide or **non-profit breeding**, non-commercial purposes: maximum three animals once yearly, or repeated import of the same microchipped animals for frequent travellers. It separately identifies regular commercial breeding, kennel, exhibit, pet transport or resale importers and requires live-animal importer accreditation. [BAI Charter, PDF pages 167–168](https://www.bai.gov.ph/cc/2024%20bai%20citizen%27s%20charter.pdf).

Current APHIS U.S.-export guidance corroborates separate personal and commercial SPSIC routes, BAI registration/approval for commercial importers, and permit-specific health conditions. Its U.S.-origin scope matters. [USDA APHIS Philippines guidance](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/pet-travel/us-to-another-country-export/pet-travel-us-philippines).

Gaps: confirm current BAI implementation of the older Charter, specific adoption/ownership-transfer cases, same-owner relocation through professional transporters, and occasional competitions. The Charter's “pet transport” wording does not justify automatically labelling every agent-assisted household move commercial.

## AE — United Arab Emirates

MOCCAE's current Arabic companion-animal service distinguishes **individuals** (maximum two pets per person per year, except returning resident pets) from **establishments**, which must hold an animal-activity licence covering companion-animal import/export or a pet-breeding centre. These are express importer/establishment conditions; the service also requires advance import permission and animal-health release documents. [MOCCAE import service, conditions and required documents](https://www.moccae.gov.ae/ar/services/import-permit-pets).

For its service/emotional-support/medical-dog documentation route, the page additionally requires an owner undertaking not to transfer ownership, abandon or breed the dog. Do not generalise this special undertaking to all pets.

Gaps: individual sale/adoption eligibility, occasional breeding or competition visits, and full commercial conditions remain unverified. Same-owner relocation is not shown as a separate health category; returning-resident exceptions need their own evidence and conditions checked.
