# Peninsular Malaysia: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft applies only to Peninsular Malaysia. DVS publishes separate Australia, Singapore and Canada protocols plus scheduled/non-scheduled rules; a December 2025 notice changes the older scheduled-country list.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Potential scheduled-country pathway under December 2025 notice

Catalogue jurisdictions: GB, IE, NZ, JP, DE, PT.

Notice classification is not proof that the pet meets 6-month residence/rabies declarations. Older index and protocol lists conflict; written DVS/MAQIS confirmation is required. [^2][^3]

### Australia-specific published protocol

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU.

Includes residence, Hendra testing and at least 10 clear days of quarantine; do not map to a generic no-quarantine group. [^5]

### Singapore-specific published protocol

Catalogue jurisdictions: SG.

A dedicated protocol exists. The 2025 notice includes Singapore as scheduled but omits it from a repeated exclusion list; resolve the inconsistency with MAQIS. [^6][^2]

### Canada-specific published protocol

Catalogue jurisdictions: CA.

Uses country-or-part rabies declarations and minimum 7-day quarantine, subject to the permit. [^7]

### Potential non-scheduled pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, FR, NL, ES, IT, CH, NO, AE, HK, CN, TW, TH, PH.

Published protocol contains demanding country-level rabies and previous-import declarations. Availability is not established merely by selecting an origin on this list. [^2][^4]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm Peninsular jurisdiction and the applicable protocol

Planning advice · Before paying for preparations or transport

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use Peninsular Malaysia's MAQIS/DVS process; Sabah and Sarawak have separate authorities and are outside this workflow.
- Submit the pet's origin and 6-month movement history for protocol confirmation. The 11 December 2025 notice adds countries including Germany and Portugal; older DVS index labels are stale.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 2. Resolve prohibited or restricted dog status

Conditional · Before applying for the import permit

Species: dog only.

- Check DVS's prohibited breeds, including pit-bull types, Akita, American Bulldog, Dogo Argentino, Fila Brasileiro, Japanese Tosa and Neapolitan Mastiff.
- Restricted breeds require prior written approval from the MAQIS Director General, pedigree/microchip evidence and the prescribed premises/owner checks.

Evidence: [^4]

### 3. Prepare the microchip and vaccination record

Required within the applicable pathway · Before veterinary certification

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an ISO-compliant microchip and records identifying the animal's breed, age, sex, colour and origin.
- Have the vet apply the selected protocol's rabies and species-specific core vaccination clauses; several published protocols require core vaccination within the previous 12 months.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 4. Obtain certifiable residence and rabies-history evidence

Required within the applicable pathway · Review the preceding 6 months before shipment

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Ask the exporting authority to verify every residence and rabies-status declaration on the chosen Malaysian certificate; do not sign a country-wide rabies-free declaration using only the pet's vaccination history.
- The Canada protocol permits a country-or-part rabies-status declaration. Singapore has its own residence and official-certification requirements; simple transit through Singapore does not establish them.

Evidence: [^7][^6][^4]

### 5. Complete the special Australia-origin conditions

Conditional · Health certificate within 7 days; Hendra sample within 14 days before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: AU.

- Document 6-month Australian residence or since birth, minimum age 3 months, and the protocol's rabies/premises-health attestations.
- Arrange the specified negative Hendra serum-neutralisation test and vaccinations. This published protocol provides at least 10 clear days of Malaysian quarantine, not a blanket Australian-origin exemption.

Evidence: [^5]

### 6. Obtain the MAQIS import permit and quarantine arrangement

Required within the applicable pathway · Before shipment; do not assume an unverified permit-validity period

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply through Dagang Net ePermit personally or through a MAQIS-registered agent; arrange quarantine space with MAQIS where required.
- Published non-scheduled and Canadian protocols require at least 7 days of quarantine, extendable up to 6 months. Scheduled and Singapore documents omit that routine clause, but obtain written confirmation for the actual permit.

Evidence: [^1][^4][^7][^3][^6]

### 7. Have the exporting authority issue the correct certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Final examination within 7 days before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the government veterinarian certify health, vaccination, identification and the protocol-specific disease/residence statements.
- Carry the official veterinary health certificate, vaccination/microchip records and Malaysian import permit; check that the animal remains clinically healthy at shipment.

Evidence: [^3][^4][^5][^7][^6]

### 8. Confirm the approved entry and inspection arrangements

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure and at the Malaysian border

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Match the flight or road route to the selected protocol and the permit's entry point; Singapore has a specific direct air/road pathway.
- Present the pet and documents to MAQIS for inspection and transfer to the agreed quarantine arrangement when required.

Evidence: [^6][^4][^1]

## Departure: exporting from Peninsular Malaysia

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Prepare the destination-specific health requirements

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arranging tests or booking travel

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination import conditions and any import permit; schedule vaccines, blood tests and treatments in its required order.
- Arrange the state DVS examination and provide the pet's identification and veterinary records for the official Veterinary Health Certificate.

Evidence: [^1]

### 2. Obtain DVS certification and the MAQIS export permit

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure; validity must match the destination and issued documents

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply for the Veterinary Health Certificate and MAQIS export permit through the stated Dagang Net process.
- Carry the official certificate, export permit and destination documents for exit inspection. This Peninsular workflow must not be reused for Sabah or Sarawak without their authority's rules.

Evidence: [^1]

## Caveats

- Draft research, not human-approved. Only Peninsular Malaysia is covered.
- The 2013-era PDFs remain linked by DVS; their continuing publication does not resolve conflicts with the newer notice or a pet-specific permit.
- Do not advertise universal no-quarantine, universal titre exemptions or a 7-day maximum stay.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Written DVS/MAQIS reconciliation is needed for the updated scheduled list, Singapore inconsistency and old country-specific protocols.
- The non-scheduled protocol's country-wide rabies-free declaration may be impossible for some listed departure countries; no substitute wording or exemption was verified.
- Current permit validity, authorised entry ports, quarantine availability and any outbreak-specific restrictions remain unverified.
- Exact species-specific vaccine clauses and restricted-breed approvals must be checked in the selected certificate.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
