# Australia: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft import workflow separates New Zealand, approved rabies-free origins and approved rabies-risk origins. Country selection alone does not establish residence, identity or permit eligibility.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Potential Group 1 pathway — New Zealand

Catalogue jurisdictions: NZ.

Requires the New Zealand guide's residence, certification and disease-history conditions; departure location alone is insufficient. [^7]

### Potential Group 2 pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: JP, SG.

Rabies-free approved origins subject to official identity and qualifying residence. Hawaii and Guam are regional Group 2 exceptions within US, not a nationwide US classification. [^1][^3]

### Potential Group 3 pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW.

Requires approved-country history, rabies preparation, official documentation and permit. Mainland US is Group 3; regional exceptions need manual handling. [^1][^4]

### No direct approved-country pathway in the published list

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, MY, PH.

A move to an approved country and its preparation/residence pathway is necessary; do not present a direct-import checklist as eligible. [^1]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Check approved-country history and prohibited animals

Planning advice · Before vaccinations, testing or booking

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use the applicable DAFF country guide and agreed export certificate; an unapproved departure country needs an approved-country preparation and residence pathway.
- Check breed and hybrid controls first. Wolf–dog hybrids and specified purebred dogs are prohibited; Bengal-cat import exemptions no longer provide an arrival pathway after 28 February 2026.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^8]

### 2. Arrange identity checks before the preparation clock

Conditional · Group 2 normally at least 180 days before export; Group 3 before RNATT if seeking shorter quarantine

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Use a readable compliant microchip on every veterinary record; have it scanned at each procedure.
- Group 2 requires official identity verification and qualifying residence; since-birth, young-animal and direct-return cases have specific exceptions.
- Group 3 official verification before the blood sample can reduce minimum quarantine from 30 to 10 days. Keep an Australian export certificate for a returning pet.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 3. Complete the Group 3 rabies sequence

Conditional · 180 days from laboratory receipt of a passing sample; RNATT valid 12 months from sampling

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Vaccinate after identification, at age 84 days or older, and keep rabies protection current. Use a government-recognised laboratory in an approved country for FAVN/RFFIT at least 0.5 IU/ml.
- Obtain the official RNATT declaration. Complete the approved-country waiting/residence conditions; permit validity cannot extend the blood test's arrival deadline.

Evidence: [^4][^5]

### 4. Obtain the permit and quarantine reservation

Conditional · Before confirming flights; allow processing and quarantine availability

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Apply through BICON with the identity and rabies documents applicable to the group. Read the issued permit before ordering remaining procedures.
- After approval, reserve Mickleham quarantine and arrange manifested air cargo to Melbourne. Group 2 has a minimum 10-day stay; ordinary Group 3 has a minimum 30-day stay unless its shorter-stay conditions are satisfied.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 5. Complete the dog's disease-specific preparation

Conditional · Most specified blood tests within 45 days of export; some preparations start earlier

Species: dog only. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Arrange Leishmania testing; intact dogs also need Brucella testing and mating restrictions. Meet the Leptospira canicola vaccination or testing option.
- Maintain vet-administered external parasite protection from at least 30 days before export. Apply canine-influenza conditions to dogs with relevant residence history, including Singapore, Canada and the USA.
- Tell the vet about any mainland-African residence: Babesia rossi treatment requirements may apply. Use the exact permit for test methods and vaccination deadlines.

Evidence: [^6][^2]

### 6. Finish parasite treatment and official certification

Conditional · Final examination within 5 days before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Cats need continuous qualifying external parasite treatment beginning at least 21 days before export. Dogs and cats need two internal treatments within 45 days, at least 14 days apart; the last is within 5 days.
- Use a government-approved vet, then the exporting authority's endorsement process. Follow the group-specific rules for originals, signatures, seals and laboratory reports; Group 2 dogs require the specified crate seal.

Evidence: [^6][^2][^4]

### 7. Use the New Zealand-specific entry pathway

Conditional · Final examination and parasite treatments within 5 days; notify DAFF at least 3 days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: NZ.

- Document residence in New Zealand since birth, direct export from Australia, or the required preceding 180 days. Previously imported dogs have additional disease-test conditions.
- Have an eligible New Zealand veterinarian complete the correct MPI certificate. Compliant pets need neither an Australian import permit nor routine quarantine under this pathway.

Evidence: [^7]

### 8. Preserve the approved route through arrival

Required within the applicable pathway · When booking and on arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For Groups 2 and 3, use Melbourne as the first Australian entry point and transfer into booked quarantine. Group 2 transhipment is limited to its approved countries and named airports.
- For New Zealand departures, arrange the direct pathway and entry inspection before any domestic transfer. Keep the pet isolated during any authorised transit and carry the original required records.

Evidence: [^2][^4][^7]

## Departure: exporting from Australia

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Obtain the destination conditions and lodge notice

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 10 working days before departure or commencement of isolation

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Get the destination's current import permit and veterinary certificate conditions before arranging tests.
- Send the Notice of Intention and supporting destination requirements to the relevant DAFF regional office; book its pre-export document assessment.

Evidence: [^9]

### 2. Complete veterinary examination and government certification

Required within the applicable pathway · Examination within 72 hours of departure, or the destination's specified period

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the private veterinarian complete the examination and health/welfare declaration; provide the owner's declaration and required test records.
- At the DAFF appointment, obtain the official health certificate and export permit. Departure must occur within 72 hours after the export permit is issued.

Evidence: [^9]

### 3. Prepare a possible Australian return before leaving

Planning advice · Before outward departure, if a return is possible

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Retain the Australian export certificate as identity evidence and check the approved-country return route.
- Discuss rabies preparation before departure; a return is not automatically exempt from import conditions simply because the pet originated in Australia.

Evidence: [^10]

## Caveats

- Draft research, not human-approved or a permit assessment.
- Group 2 and Group 3 certificate endorsement and transhipment rules are not interchangeable.
- Minimum quarantine can be extended for compliance or health issues. Carrier acceptance remains separate.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- No pet-specific BICON permit was inspected; its conditions, test-method alternatives and returning/young-pet exceptions require case review.
- Breed, pregnancy, territory, transit and commercial consignment exceptions are not exhaustive.
- Some Group 3 dog pages failed direct fetching; official indexed text and the accessible veterinary guidance were used.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
