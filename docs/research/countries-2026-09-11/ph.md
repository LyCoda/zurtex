# Philippines: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft BAI personal-pet import pathway. Approved SPSIC terms, animal age and preparation at application, official export certification and arrival inspection all matter; several exceptions on BAI's public page need case-specific clarification.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### SPSIC and official origin-attestation review

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, CN, TW, TH, MY.

No automatic rabies-vaccination exemption is assigned to a country flag. BAI must accept any origin-status attestation and any relevant history under the actual SPSIC. [^1]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm personal-pet eligibility and animal age

Required within the applicable pathway · Before applying for the SPSIC

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- The personal one-time SPSIC pathway covers up to three dogs/cats combined; the animal count must match the official health certificate. Obtain the correct BAI importer authorization for commercial or ownership-transfer cases.
- BAI states a minimum age of 120 days at application and excludes animals pregnant at application; a dam must have given birth at least 12 weeks before travel and have fitness certification.
- The same page lists younger-animal exceptions accompanying a vaccinated mother. These conflict with its minimum-age rule: obtain BAI's written acceptance before relying on an exception.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^4]

### 2. Microchip the animal and prepare identity evidence

Required within the applicable pathway · Before the import application

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Provide proof of microchip identification and a current animal photograph, with passport/owner details where required.
- Use a compatible ISO chip or bring an accepted reader for a non-standard chip, and ensure identity matches the vaccine, treatment, permit and certificate records.

Evidence: [^1]

### 3. Complete rabies vaccination before applying

Required within the applicable pathway · First vaccination at age 84 days or older and at least 14 days before SPSIC application

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Keep rabies vaccination valid through arrival and provide the vaccination evidence required by BAI. The page allows immediate travel after an annual booster when the applicable previous protection is documented.
- Only use a rabies-free-origin or non-vaccinating-country exception with the official government attestation accepted by BAI; a country flag alone does not establish a waiver.

Evidence: [^1]

### 4. Document the required canine vaccinations

Required within the applicable pathway · At least one dose at least 14 days before SPSIC application

Species: dog only.

- Provide vaccination evidence for canine distemper, infectious hepatitis, parvovirus, parainfluenza and leptospirosis.
- Ensure the licensed veterinarian records the vaccine details and animal identity, and check any additional conditions printed on the approved SPSIC.

Evidence: [^1]

### 5. Document the required feline vaccinations

Required within the applicable pathway · At least one dose at least 14 days before SPSIC application

Species: cat only.

- Provide vaccination evidence for feline panleukopenia, rhinotracheitis and calicivirus.
- Ensure the licensed veterinarian records the vaccine details and animal identity, and check any additional conditions printed on the approved SPSIC.

Evidence: [^1]

### 6. Complete parasite treatment and obtain the SPSIC

Required within the applicable pathway · Internal and external treatment 7–91 days before application; SPSIC valid for 60 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have a licensed veterinarian administer and record the required internal and external parasite treatment within BAI's stated application-based window.
- Apply through the official linked import-permit service with vaccination, treatment, microchip and identity evidence, and obtain approval before entry.
- Read the approved SPSIC's actual dates and conditions before finalizing travel; the application is not itself permission.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 7. Obtain the exporting government's health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Issued within 10 calendar days before export/departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the official Veterinary Health Certificate / Export Permit issued or endorsed by the exporting government's veterinary authority, in English or with the accepted translation.
- Include the animal and owner/importer/exporter details, purpose, origin, expected arrival and the required vaccine/treatment declarations; match the SPSIC and all animal counts.
- For a U.S. departure, use the APHIS-accredited veterinarian and USDA endorsement process currently specified for the Philippines.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 8. Present the original documents for entry inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Carry a printed approved SPSIC and original official health/export certificate and present the animal for BAI veterinary quarantine inspection.
- Provide cargo airway-bill information where applicable and complete Customs and fee formalities; missing or non-compliant documentation can result in refusal or other enforcement, not automatic release.

Evidence: [^1][^5]

## Departure: exporting from Philippines

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Complete the destination's import conditions

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arranging BAI export certification

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's current import permit, required certificate and vaccination/test/treatment conditions, including rules dependent on earlier residence or travel.
- Use a readable 15-digit chip and prepare original vaccination and destination-specific test records. Do not use the Citizen's Charter's older broad country/test table as a substitute for current destination rules.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 2. Apply to BAI NVQSD for official export certification

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure, within the destination's certificate window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Submit the original licensed-veterinarian health certificate, updated vaccination records, chip details and any required antibody or other supporting results to BAI's National Veterinary Quarantine Services Division.
- Use the current appointment/submission channel confirmed by BAI; the charter provides an in-person or official-email route. Arrange animal/chip verification and supply any additional destination declarations.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 3. Collect the BAI-endorsed export health documents

Required within the applicable pathway · After document review and required verification, before travel

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the signed and officially sealed veterinary health/export documentation from BAI; a private veterinary certificate alone is not the BAI export permission.
- Check the destination's validity window and carry the originals for departure, transit and arrival inspection. Published processing targets are estimates rather than a guaranteed departure clearance.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

## Caveats

- Draft pending human approval; the SPSIC's actual terms and BAI inspection control admission.
- BAI's minimum-age statement and young animals accompanying their mother provisions conflict internally; the application must not automatically approve those young animals.
- The public import page is undated. APHIS's U.S.-specific Philippines page was updated on 9 September 2026, but it does not resolve all BAI-page contradictions.
- Sale, adoption/ownership transfer, repeat travel, breeding and commercial movements may require a different authorization from the one-time personal-pet pathway.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- BAI must confirm any younger-animal exception and whether the intended ownership-transfer or repeat-travel purpose qualifies for a one-time SPSIC.
- The accepted rabies-free origin list and required residence/history evidence were not established sufficiently to encode country-wide exemptions.
- The current export page's destination-specific dynamic checklist was not fully retrievable; the 2024 Citizen's Charter supports the certification procedure but its broad destination-test table is not treated as current.
- Breed-specific, hybrid/wildlife and carrier restrictions require separate confirmation.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
