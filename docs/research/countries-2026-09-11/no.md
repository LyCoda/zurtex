# Norway: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

DRAFT pending owner review. Mainland Norway uses a non-commercial personal-pet pathway with origin-specific documents, dog tapeworm treatment and restricted entry points for many third countries. Its official 2026 notice says the new EU framework is not yet automatically applicable.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### EU selected origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT.

Norway's EU-origin personal-pet passport pathway applies, subject to identity, vaccination, ownership timing and dog treatment; Ireland's treatment exemption requires direct travel. [^1][^3]

### Switzerland

Catalogue jurisdictions: CH.

Accepted passport alternative and all-border-crossings exception are specifically listed by Norway; vaccination and dog treatment still apply. [^2]

### Other selected third-country origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH.

Use Norway's country guide to determine listed versus unlisted status, certificate exceptions and transit conditions. Most direct entries use Oslo Airport or Storskog. No automatic titre exemption is assigned from this grouping. [^2][^6]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm ownership, history and mainland destination

Planning advice · Before booking

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Confirm mainland Norway, pet count, no ownership transfer, and owner travel within five days of the animal; carry written authority when a companion transports it.
- Former stray dogs or cats need evidence of ownership and control for at least six months. Unknown background or a shelter history requires separate scrutiny.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^5]

### 2. Check dog breed and type restrictions

Conditional · Before booking

Species: dog only.

- Norway bans Pit Bull Terrier, American Staffordshire Terrier, Fila Brasileiro, Tosa Inu, Dogo Argentino and Czechoslovakian Wolfdog, including crosses; wolf-dog hybrids also require special attention.
- Ask the police about any suspected restricted type or exceptional case before travel. Ordinary veterinary compliance does not establish breed eligibility.

Evidence: [^4]

### 3. Confirm identity before vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Before rabies vaccination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an ISO-compatible microchip or bring a reader capable of reading a non-standard chip.
- A clearly readable tattoo is accepted only if applied before 3 July 2011; the identity marking must precede the rabies vaccination.

Evidence: [^1]

### 4. Complete and document rabies vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · First vaccination at 12 weeks or older; at least 21 days after the primary course

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have an authorised veterinarian vaccinate and document the vaccine's validity in the travel document.
- A booster given within existing validity avoids a new primary waiting period; a lapse restarts it. The Sweden-only exception is outside the selected origins.

Evidence: [^1]

### 5. Resolve the third-country titre branch

Conditional · When required: sample at least 30 days after vaccination and at least three months before entry

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH.

- For an unlisted third-country journey, obtain an approved-laboratory rabies antibody result of at least 0.5 IU/ml and carry the report.
- Listed-origin and protected-transit exceptions exist. Confirm the actual origin/transit category using Norway's current guide; missing travel or vaccine history does not establish an exception.

Evidence: [^2]

### 6. Obtain the correct passport or official certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure; within the accepted document's validity window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- From the EU, use a qualifying pet passport; third-country travel normally needs the approved health certificate. Switzerland and qualifying returning EEA pets have passport alternatives.
- Obtain government issuance or endorsement when a third-country health certificate is required and attach the owner declaration and supporting veterinary records.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^7]

### 7. Schedule the dog's tapeworm treatment

Conditional · 24–120 hours before entry; alternative recurring regime only if fully established

Species: dog only. Origin exclusions: IE only for direct journeys.

- Have a veterinarian administer and record the required Echinococcus treatment; direct travel from Ireland among the selected origins is exempt.
- Frequent EU travellers may use the documented 28-day regime: two preparatory treatments, continued maximum 28-day intervals and a final treatment after the last return. Cats do not require this treatment.

Evidence: [^3]

### 8. Use the right border crossing and present the pet

Required within the applicable pathway · Before booking; notify the veterinary border point preferably at least 48 hours ahead

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For EU arrivals and Switzerland, use the applicable border route and present the pet and records to customs; use the red channel.
- Most other third-country arrivals must enter through Oslo Airport or Storskog and undergo Norwegian Food Safety Authority checks. Ask about safeguards and notify the entry point in advance to avoid waiting.

Evidence: [^2][^8]

## Departure: exporting from Norway

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Obtain the destination-specific departure instructions

Planning advice · Well before booking veterinary appointments

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Contact the Norwegian Food Safety Authority and the destination veterinary authority about the required passport, export certificate, endorsements, tests and permits.
- Give the vet the complete itinerary and prepare the destination requirements and Norwegian return requirements together.

Evidence: [^8][^9]

### 2. Complete the document and treatments accepted by the destination

Conditional · Before departure; destination-specific vaccine and certificate deadlines

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Where the destination accepts a Norwegian pet passport, have an authorised vet check identification, vaccine validity and any timed treatments.
- Where an official export certificate is required, confirm its format and issuance appointment with the Norwegian Food Safety Authority before the final examination; no universal export deadline is established by this draft.

Evidence: [^1][^8][^9]

## Caveats

- DRAFT pending owner review. Svalbard is excluded and has separate rules.
- The 23 April 2026 official notice says Norway's existing private-pet requirements remain in force while EU-rule incorporation is considered; anticipated autumn timing is not proof of implementation.
- Commercial/ownership-transfer movements and more-than-five-pet cases outside the event exception are not covered by the ordinary pathway.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- A final date-specific check of Norwegian EEA incorporation is required before publication or travel; the retrieved official notice anticipates autumn 2026.
- Norway's linked European country list now redirects to the EU 2026 list while Norwegian guidance still cites 577/2013; exact listed/unlisted mapping must be confirmed in the current Norwegian country guide.
- The approved certificate's complete visual fields and validity instructions need checking with the issuing authority; the older official example contains image-based pages.
- The exact Norwegian non-EEA export-certificate booking/endorsement workflow was not established for each destination; the draft requires NFSA coordination rather than inventing a universal deadline.
- Airline acceptance, police exemptions, specific former-stray evidence and every transit itinerary have not been assessed.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
