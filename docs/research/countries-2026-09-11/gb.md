# Great Britain: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

DRAFT pending owner review. These steps cover England, Scotland and Wales, excluding Northern Ireland. Origin category, rabies preparation, approved routes and travel documents determine the personal-pet pathway.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### EU and accepted passport-country origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

A qualifying passport, return AHC or GB health certificate can be accepted; pet identity, vaccination, owner timing and treatment still apply. [^5][^7]

### Listed non-EU origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, TW, MY.

Ordinary first entry uses the GB health certificate without the unlisted-origin rabies test. Australian cats and Peninsular Malaysian dogs/cats have additional certificates. [^5]

### Unlisted selected origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, PH, BR.

GB health certificate plus satisfactory rabies blood test and the prescribed wait, unless a documented return exception applies. [^5][^4]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm personal travel and breed eligibility

Planning advice · Before booking

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Keep the owner's travel within five days of the pet's journey and provide written authorisation if another person accompanies it.
- Sale, rehoming, ownership transfer or more than five pets outside the event exception invoke extra rules. A banned dog type cannot enter without an existing valid Certificate of Exemption.

Evidence: [^1][^10]

### 2. Book an approved pet route

Required within the applicable pathway · Before booking; recheck shortly before departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an approved route and carrier; travel from Ireland has an approved-route exception.
- Ordinary pets arriving by air travel as cargo, with separate exceptions for qualifying assistance dogs and chartered private aircraft. Confirm carrier acceptance and handling arrangements.

Evidence: [^2]

### 3. Microchip and complete rabies vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Before travel; at least 21 full days after the primary course, potentially longer

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the microchip implanted before or with vaccination and confirm it reads correctly. The vet needs proof the pet is at least 12 weeks old for vaccination.
- Follow the vaccine's complete primary course and immunity interval; 21 days is a minimum. Keep boosters in date, since a lapse can restart preparation.

Evidence: [^3]

### 4. Complete a rabies blood test for unlisted origins

Conditional · Sample at least 30 days after vaccination; wait three months from sampling

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: CN, TH, PH.

- For a non-EU, unlisted origin, use an approved laboratory and obtain a result of at least 0.5 IU/ml.
- Carry the result and have the sampling date recorded in the travel document. Continuous vaccination preserves a satisfactory test; any return exemption must be demonstrated.

Evidence: [^4]

### 5. Obtain the correct pet travel document

Required within the applicable pathway · GB health certificate: enter within ten days of issue

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an accepted pet passport, qualifying GB-issued AHC for a return journey, or a Great Britain pet health certificate according to origin and history.
- The GB health certificate requires signature, stamp and date from an official veterinarian authorised by the exporting government; carry original records.

Evidence: [^5][^6][^7]

### 6. Add the Australian or Peninsular Malaysian health evidence

Conditional · Australia: previous 60 days; Malaysia test within ten days before departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: AU, MY.

- For cats from Australia, obtain the Australian authority's certificate that the cat had no Hendra-virus exposure in the preceding 60 days.
- For dogs or cats from Peninsular Malaysia, obtain government certification of no relevant pig contact or Nipah-affected holding in the preceding 60 days and a negative approved-laboratory Nipah antibody test.

Evidence: [^5]

### 7. Have the dog treated for tapeworm

Conditional · 24–120 hours before arrival

Species: dog only. Origin exclusions: IE, NO only for direct journeys.

- Unless arriving directly from Ireland or Norway among the selected origins, have a vet administer an approved praziquantel or equivalent Echinococcus treatment.
- The vet records the product, date, time, stamp and signature in the travel document. Recalculate the window after delays.

Evidence: [^8]

### 8. Sign the declaration and complete entry checks

Required within the applicable pathway · Before travel and at arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Sign the non-commercial declaration and carry any authorisation and evidence of the owner's travel.
- Present the pet for microchip/document checks; some checks occur before boarding. For arrivals from outside the EU, arrange customs clearance before collecting the pet.

Evidence: [^1][^9]

## Departure: exporting from Great Britain

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Prepare the EU journey and obtain an AHC

Conditional · After vaccine immunity; enter the EU within ten days of AHC issue

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination filter: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT.

- For a GB resident travelling to the EU, obtain a new AHC from an official veterinarian for each trip; current GOV.UK guidance says a pet passport cannot replace it even if EU-issued.
- The AHC supports six months of onward EU travel and re-entry to GB, subject to continuing rabies validity. Check the destination's tapeworm and arrival-point requirements.

Evidence: [^11][^12]

### 2. Arrange the destination's export certificate process

Conditional · Before departure; destination-specific validity

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, GB.

- For a non-EU destination, use the official EHC finder and its instructions; complete the export application where required and nominate the certifying official veterinarian.
- Have the vet verify the pet's identity, preparation and certificate conditions, and obtain any destination import permit. Check the exact pathway for Switzerland and Norway rather than assuming EU membership.

Evidence: [^13]

### 3. Prepare the return journey before leaving

Planning advice · Before departure; treatment can be needed 24–120 hours before return

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Check whether the countries visited alter the return blood-test or documentation pathway.
- If travelling with a dog, schedule return tapeworm treatment unless a direct-origin exception applies; keep the return document and rabies cover valid.

Evidence: [^13][^8]

## Caveats

- DRAFT pending owner review. Northern Ireland is a different jurisdiction and excluded.
- Cargo carriage can remain a non-commercial personal-pet movement when the legal purpose and owner-travel conditions are met.
- An EU-issued passport is not automatically valid for a GB resident's outbound EU trip under current GOV.UK guidance.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- The actual airline, approved arrival route, cargo handling and any transit-country requirements need journey-specific verification.
- Banned-type assessment, exemption validity and Scotland-specific dog controls require individual review; breed labels alone do not establish eligibility.
- Vaccine-brand courses, first-time versus returning travel documents and special origin certificates require document review.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
