# Portugal: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Personal dog and cat travel under the current EU framework, with national arrival procedures and export-certification contacts. Origin group and residence must be confirmed; choosing a departure country does not prove eligibility.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### EU or passport-equivalent origin

Catalogue jurisdictions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, CH, NO.

A qualifying passport pathway is available; owner residence, document validity and earlier movements still matter. [^3]

### Listed non-EU origin

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, TW, MY.

For the qualifying direct pathway, an EU rabies antibody test is not required. The official certificate, chip and valid rabies vaccination remain required; residence and transit can change the pathway. [^2][^1]

### Unlisted non-EU origin

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, PH.

Plan for an approved rabies antibody test and its waiting period unless a documented return exception applies. [^2][^1]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Resolve any restricted-dog conditions before booking

Conditional · Before choosing transport or a travel date

Species: dog only.

- Portugal has additional entry and keeping rules for potentially dangerous dogs, including certain breeds and crosses.
- Short visits and stays of four months or more have different conditions; owner accompaniment, licensing, muzzle and lead rules may apply.
- Resolve the dog’s classification and your length of stay before relying on the ordinary pet pathway.

Evidence: [^8]

### 2. Microchip your pet before the rabies vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Before the qualifying rabies vaccination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an ISO 11784/11785-compatible chip, normally a 15-digit number; if it cannot be read by the standard scanner, arrange the permitted reader alternative.
- The chip must be implanted or read before the vaccination relied on for travel. On the same day, identification must come first.
- Keep the chip number and date on the vaccination and travel documents. An old, clearly readable tattoo made before 3 July 2011 has a narrow exception.

Evidence: [^1]

### 3. Make sure the rabies vaccination is valid

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 21 days after a first completed vaccination course

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Your pet must be at least 12 weeks old when vaccinated against rabies.
- A first course needs at least 21 days before it becomes valid for travel. A booster given within the previous dose’s validity does not restart that wait.
- Use the valid-from and expiry dates in the vet’s record; protection must still be valid at entry and for any onward journey.

Evidence: [^1][^3]

### 4. Arrange the rabies blood test and waiting period

Required within the applicable pathway · Sample after the qualifying vaccination; at least 90 days before certificate issue

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: CN, TH, PH.

- For an unlisted-country pathway, use a laboratory approved by the EU and a result of at least 0.5 IU/ml.
- For a primary course, sampling is at least 30 days after completion; the current EU rule also provides for sampling within a continuously valid vaccination series.
- The sample must be at least 90 days before the animal health certificate is issued. Do not count only to the flight date.
- Keep the certified lab report. A documented successful test before leaving the EU may remove the return wait if vaccinations remain continuously valid.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 5. Book the official veterinary certificate appointment

Planning advice · Start arranging this several weeks ahead

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

- Tell the practice the first EU country you will enter and the intended arrival date.
- Confirm that an official veterinarian can issue the document, or that the authorised vet’s certificate can receive the required official endorsement.
- Bring the chip record, vaccination history and any required blood-test report. Booking early is planning advice, not an extra legal waiting period.

Evidence: [^1][^5]

### 6. Get the Animal Health Certificate issued

Conditional · Within 10 days before EU entry; issue day counts as day 1

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

- For a first non-EU entry without a qualifying passport or return-document exception, obtain the official non-commercial animal health certificate. A qualifying existing document may replace a new certificate; GB residents still need an AHC for a new EU trip.
- The certificate must cover the first EU entry point. A document signed by an unauthorised practice is not a substitute for official issue or endorsement.
- For issue from 1 October 2026, use the new EU model. Onward EU validity is up to six months from the entry check, or rabies expiry if earlier.
- Carry the signed non-commercial declaration; an authorised traveller also needs the owner’s written authority and evidence of the owner’s journey within five days.

Evidence: [^1][^4][^5]

### 7. Carry the accepted pet passport or onward travel document

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure and at every border check

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

- For a qualifying EU or passport-equivalent origin, use a valid passport issued by an authorised veterinarian.
- Check the recorded chip, owner, rabies valid-from and expiry entries. EU passport issue is tied to the owner’s residence; merely possessing a passport does not prove eligibility.
- A valid entry AHC may support onward EU travel within its validity. If vaccination or ownership circumstances have changed, confirm the correct document instead of assuming the old one still works.

Evidence: [^3][^1]

### 8. Notify the Portuguese entry point before you arrive

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 48 hours before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

- The owner or authorised traveller must send the arrival form to the designated entry point; do not assume the airline has done this.
- Include the requested travel and pet documentation and use the entry point’s own contact address.
- Lisbon and Porto have overnight inspection closures. Match your arrival to the confirmed checking arrangements.

Evidence: [^9]

### 9. Present your pet and documents at the EU entry check

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival at the first EU entry point

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO.

- Enter through a designated travellers’ point of entry and present the pet for the identity and document check.
- Carry the original certificate, supporting vaccination and test records, and owner declaration; use the customs declaration channel where required.
- The ordinary non-commercial framework is for a pet travelling with its owner, or within five days through an authorised person, without sale or transfer. Larger groups have separate rules and exceptions.

Evidence: [^1][^7][^8]

## Departure: exporting from Portugal

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Arrange the destination certificate with DGAV

Conditional · Before departure; follow the destination’s certificate and permit windows

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination exclusions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT.

- Use DGAV’s country-specific certificate library; select the correct species and destination model.
- Contact the competent local veterinary unit (UVL) before the certification visit and send the requested information.
- Keep the private vet’s records and any permit or lab reports together. The receiving country sets the examination, endorsement and arrival windows.

Evidence: [^10]

### 2. Check the passport before travelling to another EU country

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination filter: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT.

- Use the pet passport or a still-valid entry certificate that supports the onward movement.
- Keep the identification and rabies record consistent; the destination may also require a dog-specific tapeworm treatment.
- Plan your return before leaving, especially if visiting an unlisted rabies country; a pre-departure antibody test can matter for the return pathway.

Evidence: [^3][^1]

## Caveats

- One personally owned dog or cat; no sale or ownership transfer. Commercial consignments and unusual animal categories require separate handling.
- The selected arrival date is not evidence that vaccinations, residence, tests or permits are satisfied.
- Norway and Switzerland are passport-equivalent origins for EU entry; this does not make their own import rules identical to the EU.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Potentially dangerous dogs, longer stays and local registration require additional individual facts.
- No universal export-certificate expiry or processing time was inferred from country-specific forms.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
