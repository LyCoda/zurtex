# Thailand: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft DLD dog/cat import pathway. The issued DLD conditions and transport mode govern permission, official certification and arrival inspection; permission on arrival is not available for every shipment.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### DLD permit and origin-specific certificate review

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, CN, TW, MY, PH.

No automatic country-wide exemption is coded. The responsible station must assess the origin, residence/travel history, animal and transport mode against its issued conditions. [^1][^2]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Arrange DLD import permission for the transport mode

Required within the applicable pathway · Advance applications generally need at least 7 working days; permit normally valid for 60 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply to the responsible Animal Quarantine Station using R1/1 and the required passport, animal photo, identity, vaccination and transport documents; Suvarnabhumi publishes its own submission channel.
- Cargo requires advance permission. Current guidance allows eligible accompanied in-cabin or checked-pet applications at arrival when the full original documentation is present, but obtain station confirmation before relying on that route.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^4]

### 2. Identify the pet and assemble consistent records

Required within the applicable pathway · Before the permit application and health certificate

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Provide the microchip details and English vaccination records showing the animal's breed, sex, date of birth and identifying features.
- Have the veterinarian include the required vaccine and professional details and match all records to the animal and traveller; submit one complete record set per animal.

Evidence: [^2]

### 3. Complete rabies vaccination and resolve the primary-dose interval

Required within the applicable pathway · Primary rabies vaccination at age 12 weeks or older; confirm the 21-day deadline with the issuing station

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- DLD's ministry-hosted guidance places the primary/discontinuous vaccination wait at least 21 days before departure; the Thai Consulate Los Angeles page instead says 21 days before starting the permit application.
- Obtain the station's written instruction for the specific application before calculating the earliest flight. Continuously valid boosters may avoid the primary wait when the vaccination history is supplied.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 4. Complete the canine vaccination and leptospirosis conditions

Required within the applicable pathway · Core vaccination and primary-course timing per DLD; leptospirosis vaccine at least 21 days or negative test within 30 days before departure

Species: dog only.

- Document canine distemper, infectious hepatitis and parvovirus vaccination in addition to rabies.
- Provide accepted leptospirosis vaccination, or the specified negative leptospirosis test within 30 days before departure; have DLD confirm any primary-course timing conflict with the application schedule.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 5. Document the required feline vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Complete the primary vaccination interval required by the issuing station

Species: cat only.

- Provide feline panleukopenia vaccination evidence in addition to rabies.
- Keep the vaccination record and any continuous booster history available for the permit review; do not assume that a recently administered first dose meets the travel requirement.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 6. Obtain the official English health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure; use the validity window in DLD's approved conditions

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the exporting government's veterinary authority issue or endorse the health certificate and verify that it contains the required identity and health declarations.
- Carry the required original official certificate, vaccination records, passport and printed permission. For U.S. exports, the current consular instruction requires arrival within 10 days of USDA endorsement; do not generalize that interval to every origin without confirmation.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 7. Present the pet to the Animal Quarantine Station and Customs

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival, during the applicable passenger or cargo service hours

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Take the pet and original documents to the arrival Animal Quarantine Station for inspection and the applicable R6/R7 processing.
- Complete Customs declaration and charges where applicable. Cargo clearance hours differ from accompanied-pet service; plan the itinerary with the station and do not assume release without inspection or further quarantine.

Evidence: [^4][^3]

## Departure: exporting from Thailand

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Complete the destination's import conditions

Required within the applicable pathway · Before reserving the export inspection

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's permit and official certificate requirements and complete the required chip, vaccinations, laboratory tests and treatments.
- Contact the DLD Animal Quarantine Station handling departure about its application and examination arrangements, particularly for cargo or ownership-transfer movements.

Evidence: [^4]

### 2. Arrange the DLD examination and export documentation

Required within the applicable pathway · For Suvarnabhumi accompanied pets: no more than 3 days before departure; confirm other ports separately

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Bring the pet, passport and vaccination evidence, with all additional destination documents, to the responsible station's export health check.
- At Suvarnabhumi, the official airport guidance directs the traveller to the Animal Quarantine Station for the health certificate and export permit; do not treat the three-day instruction as a nationwide deadline.

Evidence: [^4]

### 3. Carry the official certificate and export permit

Required within the applicable pathway · Before airline acceptance and departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Check that the DLD health certificate/export permit and any required destination form identify the animal consistently and remain valid on arrival.
- Complete the airline and Customs departure formalities applicable to the movement, keeping original certification available for transit and destination inspection.

Evidence: [^4]

## Caveats

- Draft pending human approval; no quarantine exemption or same-day release is promised.
- The current Los Angeles consular page says no dog breeds are banned from importation, contradicting older commonly repeated Pit Bull/AmStaff bans; airline restrictions remain separate.
- The detailed consular ten-day health-certificate instruction is U.S.-specific, not a verified global rule.
- Sale, adoption/ownership transfer and commercial cargo may require additional trade handling beyond this personal-pet workflow.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- The official 21-day primary vaccination instructions conflict on application versus departure; the permit-issuing station must resolve the case.
- A current universal non-U.S. health-certificate validity period and national export application deadline were not verified.
- Nationwide breed legislation, station-specific arrival quarantine decisions and non-Suvarnabhumi export procedures require confirmation.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
