# Taiwan: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft APHIA import pathway. Recognized rabies-free residence, other-origin quarantine and the separately approved quarantine-waiver route have different evidence and timing requirements.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Recognized rabies-free jurisdictions in the catalogue

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU, JP, NZ, NO, SG, GB.

Norway excludes Svalbard; residence and controlled-transit criteria must also be met. Hawaii and Guam are qualifying US regions, not a US-wide exemption. [^1]

### Other-origin quarantine or separately approved waiver route

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, AE, HK, CN, TH, MY, PH.

US excludes qualifying Hawaii/Guam cases. Rabies-free departure without the required history also falls into this route; Mainland China has additional trade permission. [^1]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Establish the animal's rabies-status and residence pathway

Required within the applicable pathway · Before choosing vaccination, testing and travel dates

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For a recognized rabies-free jurisdiction, document the required continuous residence: since birth for a young animal, the preceding 180 days for an older animal, or the applicable continuous residence since Taiwan export.
- If the residence or transit conditions are not met, use the rabies-infected-origin pathway even when the departure airport is in a recognized jurisdiction. Retain prior Taiwan export documents for any return exception.

Evidence: [^1]

### 2. Microchip and document the required rabies vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Rabies vaccination at age 90 days or older; 30 days–1 year before import

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Record a readable microchip consistently across the vaccination, test and official health documents.
- Use an inactivated vaccine or another vaccine specifically accepted by Taiwan, and keep the required vaccination valid for the planned import date.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 3. Prepare antibody evidence for the infected-origin pathway

Conditional · Ordinary pathway: blood sampling 90 days–1 year before import

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an accepted laboratory and obtain a rabies antibody result of at least 0.5 IU/ml with matching animal identification.
- Prepare for the ordinary seven-day quarantine pathway unless APHIA approves a waiver. Repeat-test chains and animals previously exported from Taiwan have separate evidence rules; do not automatically restart or waive the wait.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 4. Apply for a quarantine waiver if its additional conditions are met

Conditional · Ordinary waiver evidence: sample 180 days–1 year before import; apply at least 120 days ahead, or use the approved direct-report route with at least 20 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For the standard waiver route, submit the qualifying test and complete vaccination/history evidence to APHIA with the early application.
- For an application closer than 120 days but at least 20 days before import, arrange the specified direct verification from the laboratory or exporting authority. Taiwan pre-export tests and continuous repeat-test histories have separate waiver rules.
- Wait for APHIA's actual waiver decision; a passing result or long wait alone is not a quarantine exemption.

Evidence: [^1]

### 5. Obtain the additional Mainland China import authorization

Conditional · Before the animal-quarantine permit application

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: CN.

- For an animal from Mainland China without the qualifying Taiwan export documentation, obtain the additional Ministry of Economic Affairs / International Trade Administration authorization.
- Confirm the required project-consent and import-permit sequence with the trade authority and match the animal's chip and identity across both permissions; the FAQ restricts this special route to eligible residents.

Evidence: [^1][^3]

### 6. Obtain APHIA permission and a quarantine place where required

Required within the applicable pathway · Apply at least 20 days before import; earlier for waiver verification or special cases

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply through the pet e-permit system with identity, vaccination and applicable laboratory/history documents, choosing the appropriate quarantine or waiver pathway.
- Secure any required quarantine reservation and travel within the dates on the approved permit. Do not infer availability or permit validity from a proposed flight.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^4]

### 7. Obtain the official export health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Within the destination certificate's applicable validity window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the exporting government's veterinary authority issue or endorse the required Taiwan certificate with chip, vaccination, test and eligible-origin declarations.
- Carry the original certificate, import permit and supporting records. Verify the currently accepted certificate/permit version with APHIA, especially during the 2026 form transition.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 8. Preserve transit eligibility and present the animal for inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · During transit and on arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- If a rabies-free-route animal transits an infected jurisdiction, use APHIA's accepted sealed-crate or official/airline transit certification procedure proving the required airport and animal-contact controls.
- Declare the animal to APHIA on arrival, present the original documents and attend chip/document inspection and any ordered quarantine.
- American Pit Bull Terrier, American Pit Bull and American Staffordshire Terrier are prohibited. Bengal cats need accepted F5-or-later ancestry evidence for the ordinary domestic-cat route; earlier generations are not ordinary pet imports.

Evidence: [^1]

## Departure: exporting from Taiwan

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Check destination certification and Taiwan re-entry needs

Required within the applicable pathway · Before scheduling vaccines, testing or export inspection

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's import permit, certificate template and health conditions, and complete its vaccination, test and treatment sequence.
- If a return to Taiwan is possible, retain export proof and discuss pre-export rabies testing and re-entry eligibility before leaving.

Evidence: [^5][^1]

### 2. Arrange APHIA export quarantine certification when required

Conditional · Plan an examination within about 1 week before export, but follow the destination's exact window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- APHIA's current guidance calls for export quarantine where the destination requires Taiwan's official certificate or the animal may return to Taiwan; contact a branch or inspection station, not the head office, for the examination.
- Bring the animal, owner identification, original vaccination and other destination evidence, import permit/template and transport details. A representative needs the required authorization and identification.

Evidence: [^5]

### 3. Collect and check the official export certificate

Conditional · After a satisfactory APHIA examination and before departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the official certificate needed for the destination or later Taiwan re-entry, checking the animal's identification and endorsements.
- APHIA states that its export certificate has no single universal expiry; the destination's time limit controls. Older guidance that presents seven days as an absolute universal deadline should not override the updated page.

Evidence: [^5]

## Caveats

- Draft pending human approval. The 90-day sampling baseline and the 180-day waiver pathway are distinct and must not be combined into a single waiting rule.
- The current APHIA FAQ identifies additional medical and special-project procedures; these are not ordinary household-pet exemptions.
- APHIS's U.S. summary describes the seven-day quarantine route but does not fully describe the newer APHIA waiver options; APHIA's current conditions control.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Some APHIA canonical pages timed out. The official e-permit FAQ PDF was readable; the current export page was read on APHIA's faw subdomain.
- The 2026 certificate/permit transition was identified in APHIA search results, but the underlying revised form could not be fully inspected; verify the actual accepted version before issuing documents.
- Mainland's additional trade procedure was corroborated by the current FAQ and official search text, but its dedicated page was not fully accessible.
- Species/breed ancestry, special medical quarantine and commercial/ownership-transfer cases need direct APHIA review.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
