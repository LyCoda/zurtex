# Japan: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft AQS personal dog/cat import pathway. Designated-region eligibility depends on continuous residence and transit history; all animals undergo import inspection.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Designated region, subject to residence and transit proof

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU, NZ.

These country flags are insufficient alone: the designated-region residence and transit conditions must be met. Hawaii and Guam are additional regional exceptions within US. [^2]

### Non-designated jurisdiction pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, SG, HK, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH, US, BR.

US excludes Hawaii and Guam when their designated-region conditions are met. Rabies vaccination, approved testing and the waiting period apply unless AQS confirms a specific return exception. [^3]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Establish designated-region residence eligibility

Required within the applicable pathway · Before selecting the preparation pathway

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- The designated regions are Australia, New Zealand, Iceland, Fiji, Hawaii and Guam. Other parts of the United States are not designated.
- For the designated pathway, establish residence since birth, continuously for the 180 days immediately before export, or continuously since direct export from Japan; otherwise use AQS's non-designated pathway.

Evidence: [^2]

### 2. Microchip the animal before rabies preparation

Required within the applicable pathway · Before the first qualifying rabies vaccination; same-day implantation before vaccination is accepted

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an ISO 11784/11785 15-digit microchip and have the veterinarian verify it when recording each procedure.
- Consult AQS before using a non-ISO chip and arrange its reader if accepted. AQS has special notices concerning invalid or duplicate chip numbers; obtain an identity review if affected.

Evidence: [^3][^1]

### 3. Complete qualifying rabies vaccinations

Conditional · First dose at age 91 days or older; second at least 30 days later and within the first dose's validity

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Required for the non-designated pathway, including animals departing a designated region without its required residence history.
- Use accepted inactivated or recombinant rabies vaccines after microchipping; live and RNA vaccines are not accepted. Keep vaccinations continuously valid through arrival; a lapse can require restarting the sequence.

Evidence: [^3]

### 4. Obtain the antibody result and complete the waiting period

Conditional · Sample after the second qualifying dose; arrival at least 180 days after sampling, with sampling day counted as day zero

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For the non-designated pathway, use an AQS-designated laboratory and obtain a result of at least 0.5 IU/ml; arrival must remain within the test's two-year validity and valid rabies coverage.
- If arriving before the 180-day wait is complete, quarantine can cover the shortfall. Retesting and return-to-Japan exceptions require AQS assessment rather than an automatic new date calculation.

Evidence: [^3]

### 5. Notify the arrival Animal Quarantine Service

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 40 days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Submit the advance notification through NACCS or the applicable AQS office, with the animal, route and preparation details.
- Obtain the Approval of Import Inspection and report changes; an earlier arrival or different port may not be accepted merely by changing the flight booking.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 6. Obtain the government-endorsed health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Clinical examination within 10 days before boarding

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have a veterinarian examine dogs and cats for rabies and dogs additionally for leptospirosis; use the applicable AQS certificate, normally Form AC for non-designated movements.
- Obtain issuance or endorsement by the exporting government's veterinary authority and ensure chip, vaccine, blood-test and residence details are complete. Send a draft to AQS in advance as a planning check.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 7. Preserve eligible transit conditions and use an accepted port

Required within the applicable pathway · Before confirming the itinerary and during transit

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For the designated-region pathway transiting a non-designated region, arrange the required sealed transport or official transit certificate; an ordinary connection does not preserve eligibility automatically.
- Dogs must enter through a designated dog-import port. Ask AQS about the intended port and transport arrangements, especially for cats using another port.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 8. Present the animal and documents for import inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival; dog registration within 30 days after import

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Present the animal for microchip verification and inspection with the required original or officially accepted electronic certificates, approval and application documents.
- AQS says compliant inspections can finish within 12 hours, but deficiencies can lead to extended quarantine or return; do not treat this as a guaranteed release time. Register an imported dog with the municipality and follow Japanese rabies requirements.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

## Departure: exporting from Japan

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Complete the destination's import preparation

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arranging the AQS examination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's current import conditions, permit and official certificate template, including any history-dependent rabies rules.
- Complete the destination's required vaccination, tests and treatment and plan Japanese re-entry separately if the animal will return.

Evidence: [^4]

### 2. Apply to AQS for export inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 14 days before the desired inspection date

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply through NACCS or the responsible AQS office and submit the supporting vaccination, test and destination documents.
- Arrange the inspection appointment after applying; the current AQS instruction is 14 days, not the seven-day period in older guidance.

Evidence: [^4]

### 3. Attend the export examination and collect the AQS certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Within 10 days before departure, unless the destination specifies a different examination window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Bring the animal and original supporting documents; provide a power of attorney if a representative attends.
- After a satisfactory examination and document review, obtain the AQS export quarantine certificate and any destination-specific certification; carry these for the journey.

Evidence: [^4]

## Caveats

- Draft pending human approval; no arrival or quarantine outcome is guaranteed.
- Both dogs and cats require inspection, including assistance dogs.
- Biobest reports issued from 27 November 2024 through 25 June 2025 require AQS review following the laboratory's removal from the approved list.
- Personal and commercial movements can have additional customs or ownership requirements beyond this animal-quarantine workflow.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Prior Japan export, repeated titer tests, interrupted vaccination histories and flagged microchip series require individual AQS review.
- Current airline breed restrictions, municipal dog rules and commercial transfer requirements are not exhaustively covered.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
