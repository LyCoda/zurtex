# New Zealand: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft 2026-standard pathway with an explicit transition warning: MPI also accepts the 2021 standard until 1 April 2027. Keep each pet on a coherent standard and certificate set.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Potential Category 1 — Australia

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU.

Use the Australia certificate pathway and document the pet's history; not a general exemption based solely on the last flight. [^3][^4]

### Potential Category 2

Catalogue jurisdictions: JP, SG.

Qualifying residence and official identity conditions apply. Hawaii and Guam are separate regional entries, not all of the US. [^3][^2]

### Potential Category 3

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW, MY.

Eligibility depends on the chosen standard, history, rabies sequence and certificate. Malaysia's approved geographical scope must match the actual location. [^3][^2]

### No direct categorised-country pathway reviewed

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, PH.

Do not infer that a short stay in a listed country establishes eligibility. Obtain an MPI-approved preparation pathway. [^3]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Select the standard and verify origin history

Planning advice · Before starting official identification or blood testing

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- The 2026 standard took effect on 1 July 2026; the 2021 standard remains an alternative until 1 April 2027. Obtain the forms for the chosen pathway.
- Check MPI's categorised-country list against residence and travel history. China, Thailand and the Philippines are not direct categorised origins in the reviewed list.

Evidence: [^1][^3]

### 2. Check identity and animal eligibility

Required within the applicable pathway · Before veterinary preparations

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use the pet's microchip consistently in every examination, vaccination, treatment and test record.
- Resolve prohibited dog breeds and hybrid-cat eligibility before booking; pregnancy must not exceed 42 days at shipment.

Evidence: [^4][^2]

### 3. Complete the applicable official identity sequence

Conditional · Timing depends on category, age and RNATT age

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW, MY. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Category 2 normally requires an official scan at least 6 months before shipment; animals younger than 6 months have a specific pre-preparation exception.
- Category 3: if RNATT is 6–12 months old, official identification precedes sampling; if 3–under 6 months old, two scans are required, the first at least 6 months before shipment and the second before sampling.
- The exporting authority sends verification directly to MPI. NZ-origin pets may use the original NZ export certificate/AWEC exemption.

Evidence: [^2]

### 4. Meet Category 3 rabies requirements

Conditional · Primary vaccination at least 6 months before shipment; RNATT 3–12 calendar months before shipment

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW, MY. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Rabies vaccination must follow identification, start no earlier than 12 weeks of age and remain valid through shipment.
- Use the prescribed laboratory test with a result of at least 0.5 IU/ml. The 2026 standard's calendar-month windows must not be replaced with an assumed 90-day rule.

Evidence: [^2]

### 5. Book quarantine and apply for the import permit

Conditional · Apply at least 30 working days before the permit is needed

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: JP, SG, US, CA, GB, IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, AE, HK, TW, MY. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Reserve an MPI-approved quarantine facility and include its confirmation with the permit application.
- Category 2 and 3 pets ordinarily undergo at least 10 days of quarantine. Category 2 residence must meet the guide's 6-month, since-birth or qualifying-return conditions unless its rabies alternative applies.

Evidence: [^4]

### 6. Complete the chosen standard's treatments and tests

Required within the applicable pathway · Work backwards from shipment using the selected certificate

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Under the ordinary 2026 pathway, give two veterinary internal-parasite treatments: first within 30 days, at least 14 days before the second; second within 5 days of shipment. Qualifying NZ-origin pets overseas for 30 days or less have a separate single-treatment provision.
- For dogs, have the certifying vet select the applicable Babesia, Brucella, Leishmania, heartworm, leptospirosis and influenza requirements using age, vaccination and country history. Complete external-parasite treatment exactly as the chosen standard specifies.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 7. Obtain the official export certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · 2026-standard final inspection within 2 days before shipment

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the authorised veterinarian complete the destination certificate and arrange official endorsement through the exporting authority.
- Carry the correct standard's certificate, permit where required, laboratory reports and identification evidence; do not combine old and new certificate clauses.

Evidence: [^2][^5][^1]

### 8. Arrange the entry inspection and handover

Required within the applicable pathway · Australia pathway: notify MPI at least 5 working days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Air arrivals are through Auckland or Christchurch. For quarantine arrivals, coordinate notification and collection with the booked facility.
- Compliant pets from Australia generally need no import permit or routine quarantine. Present them for MPI inspection and declare medication with its supporting prescription.

Evidence: [^4]

## Departure: exporting from New Zealand

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Plan against the destination's import standard

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arranging vaccinations, tests or travel

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination requirements and import permit, using the current MPI Overseas Market Access Requirements where available.
- Use a New Zealand registered veterinarian to prepare the pet and retain microchip, vaccine and test records. Confirm whether the carrier requires a pet exporter.

Evidence: [^6]

### 2. Arrange MPI certification and welfare documentation

Conditional · Book before departure; examination/test windows depend on destination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination exclusions: AU.

- Provide the veterinarian's and owner's supporting declarations and destination documents to MPI for official certification.
- Obtain the required export certificate and, when applicable, Animal Welfare Export Certificate through the appropriate departure office. Do not assume all countries use one universal validity period.

Evidence: [^6]

### 3. Use the special New Zealand-to-Australia certificate process

Conditional · Final examination and parasite treatment within 5 days; Australian notification at least 3 days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Destination filter: AU.

- Use the current Australia OMAR and an eligible MPI-listed practice or MPI official veterinarian for the applicable AUPET certificate.
- Check residence and previously imported-dog testing conditions, and arrange the direct Australian entry inspection; retain original export records for any return.

Evidence: [^7][^8]

## Caveats

- Draft research; 2026-standard steps are not a human-approved assessment.
- The 2021 and 2026 pathways coexist until the transition deadline. Rules cannot be mixed to obtain the shortest apparent timeline.
- Dog breed/hybrid restrictions and special short-return cases require individual review.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- MPI document downloads returned 403 errors; final-standard clauses and government guide text were read through official indexed content. Re-open the final IHS and certificates before approval.
- All dog-panel sampling windows, external-parasite details and category-specific attestations are not reproduced here.
- The MPI general webpage simplifies identification to 6 months; the final IHS contains the category, RNATT-age and young-pet exceptions retained above.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
