# Hong Kong: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft personal-pet pathway. AFCD permission, the animal's eligible origin/history and the permit's individual terms determine admission; quarantine exemption is not automatic.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### AFCD Group I

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU, IE, JP, NZ, GB.

Quarantine exemption remains conditional on the applicable residence/history, health and permit requirements. Hawaii is a separate eligible region of US, not a country-wide US classification. [^1][^2]

### AFCD Group II

Catalogue jurisdictions: CA, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, CH, NO, SG, TW, US.

US entry here means continental USA, not every US territory. Eligibility requires the permit's residence and local rabies-history conditions; all other requirements must also be satisfied. [^1][^3]

### AFCD Group IIIA

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, MY.

Mainland China entered this group on 3 June 2025. The current rabies/test procedure-location rules and a minimum 30-day quarantine pathway apply; departure from this group alone does not validate prior procedures. [^1][^5]

### AFCD Group IIIB

Catalogue jurisdictions: AE, PH.

Ordinary pathway requires a booked quarantine place and at least 120 days' quarantine, subject to the issued permit and inspection. [^1][^6]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Establish the applicable AFCD origin group

Required within the applicable pathway · Before vaccines, tests and travel bookings

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Match the exporting jurisdiction and the animal's residence and movement history to the current AFCD group; a departure airport alone does not establish Group I or II eligibility.
- Group I normally requires 180 continuous days' residence, residence since birth, or continuous residence since direct import from Hong Kong. Group II requires 180 days or since birth plus the required local rabies-history declaration.
- Use the permit pathway for the actual movement purpose; traders require the applicable Animal Trader Licence before applying.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 2. Obtain the Special Permit and any quarantine reservation

Required within the applicable pathway · Before shipment; follow the allocated arrival date

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply using AF240 and the required identity/declaration documents; use AF301 where the Mainland China application route requires it.
- For Group IIIA or IIIB, arrange an approved quarantine place first and submit the reservation documentation with the application.
- Do not ship before permission is granted. Group IIIA ordinarily requires at least 30 days' quarantine and Group IIIB at least 120 days; detention can be extended.

Evidence: [^1][^4][^6]

### 3. Identify the animal and check age and breed restrictions

Required within the applicable pathway · Before certification and, for Group IIIA, before rabies vaccination and blood sampling

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use the microchip identification accepted by the applicable permit and ensure every certificate carries the same number; arrange a suitable reader for a non-standard chip if accepted.
- Group I animals must be at least 60 days old; Group II animals at least five months old. Check pregnancy and other eligibility conditions on the issued permit.
- Pit Bull Terrier including American Staffordshire Terrier, Japanese Tosa, Dogo Argentino, Fila Brasileiro and their crosses are prohibited; Staffordshire Bull Terriers require the specified breed declaration.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3][^5]

### 4. Complete the group's rabies vaccination and testing conditions

Conditional · Group II/IIIA vaccination: 30 days–1 year before export; IIIA blood sample: 90 days–1 year before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For Group II and IIIA, use an accepted inactivated or recombinant rabies vaccine; the primary dose must be administered at age 90 days or older.
- Group IIIA additionally requires an approved-laboratory FAVN or RFFIT result of at least 0.5 IU/ml. After a primary vaccination, wait at least 30 days before sampling.
- For Group IIIA, chip implantation must precede vaccination and sampling, and those procedures must be performed by a registered veterinarian in Hong Kong or an AFCD Group I, II or IIIA jurisdiction.

Evidence: [^3][^5]

### 5. Complete the required canine core vaccinations

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 14 days and no more than 1 year before export, subject to the permit's full-course conditions

Species: dog only.

- Document vaccination against canine distemper, infectious canine hepatitis and canine parvovirus.
- Have the veterinarian confirm the required course is complete and record vaccine details against the dog's microchip; rabies is a separate group-dependent condition.

Evidence: [^2][^3][^5][^6]

### 6. Complete the required feline core vaccinations

Required within the applicable pathway · At least 14 days and no more than 1 year before export, subject to the permit's full-course conditions

Species: cat only.

- Document vaccination against feline panleukopenia and the feline respiratory disease complex.
- Have the veterinarian confirm the full required course and match the vaccination record to the cat's microchip. Obtain separate AFCD confirmation for hybrid cats; Bengal F5-or-later evidence is relevant, while Savannah cats are not covered by ordinary dog/cat permit terms.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3][^5][^6]

### 7. Obtain the permit-specific animal health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Group I/II: within 14 days before export; Group IIIA: within 7 days; Group IIIB: within 14 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use the certificate and declarations specified in the Special Permit, recording the animal's identity, health, vaccination and applicable residence/test history.
- Group I, II and IIIA certificates require issuance or endorsement by the exporting government's veterinary authority. Follow Group IIIB's stated government or registered-veterinarian certification terms.
- Carry the originals required by the permit, including the Special Permit, vaccination records and any antibody-test report.

Evidence: [^2][^3][^5][^6]

### 8. Arrange approved transport and AFCD arrival inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · Notify AFCD at least 24 hours before arrival during office hours

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- The ordinary air-import route is manifested cargo using the most direct routing; obtain advance approval for stopovers or conveyances that the permit restricts.
- Obtain the required airline declaration, including crate, transit-area and contact controls. For Mainland land entry, use the designated control point and operating hours authorized by AFCD.
- Present the animal and original documents for inspection and any quarantine transfer. Dogs aged five months or above need the Hong Kong licensing and rabies arrangements required at arrival.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3][^5]

## Departure: exporting from Hong Kong

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Arrange the destination's import documents and veterinary work

Required within the applicable pathway · Before fixing the veterinary and flight dates

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's permit, certificate template and current entry conditions, including requirements based on the animal's earlier residence and travel.
- Book vaccinations, laboratory tests and treatments to meet that destination's sequence and validity periods; consider Hong Kong re-entry requirements before leaving.

Evidence: [^7]

### 2. Obtain AFCD certification or endorsement

Conditional · Private-vet certificate normally within 10 days before departure, unless the destination specifies otherwise

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Where official certification is required, submit the original private-vet health certificate, supporting vaccination/test records, import permit and dog licence as applicable with the AFCD application and fee.
- If the destination specifically requires a government-veterinarian examination, arrange the AFCD appointment at least 10 working days before the expected examination.
- AFCD describes ordinary endorsement as one working day and government certificate issue as two working days after complete processing; these are service estimates, not a guarantee or the destination's validity period.

Evidence: [^7]

### 3. Resolve hybrid-cat and wildlife export controls

Conditional · Before applying for export certification

Species: cat only.

- For Bengal, Savannah or similar hybrid cats, supply the ancestry documentation required to establish the applicable domestic-cat or controlled-wildlife pathway.
- Obtain any required CITES or other permits separately; an ordinary pet health certificate does not replace wildlife authorization.

Evidence: [^7]

## Caveats

- All records are draft and pending human approval; none constitutes AFCD approval or a quarantine-release guarantee.
- Assistance-dog concessions are a separate approval scheme; emotional-support status is not a general exemption.
- The September 2026 Group IIIA PDF is newer than earlier cached permit terms and must take precedence.
- The MY catalogue is limited to Peninsular Malaysia; obtain confirmation for any wider Malaysian origin or transit history.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Case-specific residence exceptions, re-import concessions and unusual transit histories need AFCD confirmation.
- Exact quarantine availability, charges, arrival slot, pregnancy eligibility outside the cited Group I/II terms and non-standard species/breed cases must be checked on the issued permit.
- Commercial transfers and assistance-dog cases are not exhaustively modelled.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
