Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

DRAFT pending owner review. Switzerland distinguishes recognised European pet-passport origins, low-rabies-risk third countries and rabies-risk third countries. Direct air entry from a rabies-risk origin adds an FSVO import licence.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### EU and recognised European passport origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: IE, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, PT, NO.

Recognised-passport pathway, subject to identification, rabies validity and the personal-movement rules. [^3][^1]

### Low-risk third-country origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, GB, AE, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ, TW, MY.

Current FSVO June 2026 list places these in the low-risk category; first-entry certification and route controls still apply. [^3][^1]

### Rabies-risk selected origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: CN, TH, PH.

Not listed among low-risk origins; titre/wait and direct-air licence branches apply, subject to documented exemptions and transit history. [^3][^1][^4]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm the personal-pet and origin pathway

Planning advice · Before booking

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Record ownership, origin and transit history, pet count and the owner's travel dates; transfer to another person requires a different process.
- An authorised companion needs the owner's written declaration, with owner travel within five days. Use FSVO's entry check and current rabies-country list.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 2. Check cropped, docked and restricted animals

Conditional · Before booking

Species: dog only.

- Dogs with docked tails or cropped ears are subject to an import prohibition; ask FSVO to determine any applicable temporary-visit or relocation exception before travel.
- Check the destination canton's dog rules and any CITES requirements for protected species or hybrids.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 3. Confirm identification before rabies preparation

Required within the applicable pathway · Before vaccination or verification of an existing vaccination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the pet's microchip scanned and match the number to vaccination and travel records.
- Identification must precede the rabies vaccination relied on for entry; a document alone does not establish the required order.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 4. Complete rabies vaccination or obtain a specific exception

Required within the applicable pathway · Ordinary primary dose from 12 weeks; at least 21 days before valid entry

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Keep rabies vaccination valid throughout entry and check the completed primary course.
- FSVO describes young-animal declaration pathways and narrowly justified medical permits. These are conditional exceptions, not automatic permission for an unvaccinated animal.

Evidence: [^1]

### 5. Complete rabies-risk blood testing and waiting

Conditional · At least 30 days after primary vaccination; at least 90 days before certificate issue

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: CN, TH, PH.

- For rabies-risk origins, obtain a designated-laboratory result of at least 0.5 IU/ml and preserve uninterrupted booster cover.
- The current FSVO-linked 2026 certificate measures the 90-day interval to certificate issue. Sampling during a continuously valid vaccine series has a specific exception to the post-primary interval; confirm with the official certifier.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 6. Obtain the direct-air rabies-risk import licence

Conditional · Before booking final air arrangements and before entry

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: CN, TH, PH.

- For direct air entry from a rabies-risk country, apply to FSVO for the required import licence.
- Confirm the permitted entry airport and licence conditions; entry through an EU country also requires compliance at that first border.

Evidence: [^1]

### 7. Obtain the accepted passport or official certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Third-country certificate: documentary/identity check within ten days of issue or endorsement

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use the recognised pet passport when that pathway applies; a first-time third-country entry generally needs the official exporting-country veterinary certificate.
- Use the current FSVO-linked model and required owner declaration, with certified identification, vaccination and any laboratory evidence.

Evidence: [^1][^4]

### 8. Use the permitted airport and declare the pet

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival; arrange handling beforehand

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For direct air travel, FSVO lists accompanied pets at Basel, Geneva or Zurich, and unaccompanied air-waybill movements at Geneva or Zurich; permit-dependent cases need specific airport confirmation.
- Present third-country pets and documents at the red customs exit for the mandatory entry control.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

## Departure: exporting from Switzerland

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Check the destination and return requirements

Planning advice · Before booking

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's identification, vaccination, permit, testing and certificate requirements and include any transit country.
- Prepare return requirements before leaving, especially rabies-risk testing and any direct-air re-entry licence.

Evidence: [^2][^1]

### 2. Arrange the passport or cantonal veterinary certificate

Conditional · Within the destination's document window; before departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For destinations accepting it, have a Swiss-authorised veterinary practice complete the pet passport and relevant vaccination records.
- If an official veterinary export certificate is needed, obtain it through the responsible cantonal veterinary service and follow the destination's format and timing.

Evidence: [^1]

## Caveats

- DRAFT pending owner review; entry-check and permit decisions remain individual.
- Switzerland is not an EU member; the current FSVO certificate and Swiss airport/licence conditions must be used.
- Cargo mode does not alone determine commercial purpose, but air-waybill entry affects permitted Swiss airports.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Cropped/docked-dog exceptions, all cantonal breed controls, individual medical/young-animal exemptions and licence processing times are not fully assessed.
- Exact bilateral transit paths and airline acceptance have not been assessed.
- FSVO's general page describes under-seven-month rabies-risk entry as prohibited; use the current certificate's date arithmetic and confirm any apparent age-boundary discrepancy with FSVO.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
