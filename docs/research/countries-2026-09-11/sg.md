# Singapore: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft workflow uses AVS Schedules I, II and III and the current dog-and-cat licensing process. Residency and the owner's travel history can change the quarantine pathway.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Potential Schedule I pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU, NZ, IE, GB.

Requires Schedule I residence and certification, not merely a departure from a listed airport. [^2]

### Potential Schedule II pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, FR, DE, HK, IT, JP, NO, PT, ES, CH, NL.

Requires qualifying residence and rabies preparation. Spain excludes Ceuta and Melilla; quarantine also depends on ownership and owner-entry timing. [^1][^3]

### Schedule III pathway for these listed origins

Catalogue jurisdictions: AE, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH.

Other-origin conditions include rabies preparation and minimum 30-day AQC quarantine; history can require case-specific assessment. [^1][^4]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Check pet, housing and microchip eligibility

Required within the applicable pathway · Before preparations or reservations

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Identify the pet with an ISO 11784/11785 Annex A microchip and use that number throughout its records; it must be at least 12 weeks old at export.
- Check AVS prohibited breeds and the intended home's pet rules. Early-generation Bengal and Savannah cats require separate eligibility review; do not assume every hybrid is allowed.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 2. Document the correct schedule and residence history

Required within the applicable pathway · Document the preceding 6 months, or since birth when applicable

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For Schedule I, establish qualifying residence in Schedule I countries; for Schedule II, establish qualifying residence in Schedule I/II countries.
- Use the applicable AVS conditions and certificate. An airport connection or recent relocation does not automatically confer the departure country's lower-risk treatment.

Evidence: [^2][^3]

### 3. Complete rabies vaccination and antibody testing

Conditional · Sample at least 28 days after vaccination; at least 90 days and within 12 months before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: US, CA, FR, DE, HK, IT, JP, NO, PT, ES, CH, NL, AE, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH. Retained as a conditional alternative if lower-risk residence or return history is not established.

- Keep rabies vaccination current and obtain a titre of at least 0.5 IU/ml.
- Use a WOAH reference laboratory or an approved laboratory in a Schedule I/II country, with the microchip recorded on the report.

Evidence: [^3][^4]

### 4. Complete the species-specific vaccination course

Required within the applicable pathway · Finish the required primary course at least 2 weeks before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Dogs need distemper, adenovirus type 1 and parvovirus protection; cats need calicivirus, herpesvirus type 1 and panleukopenia protection.
- Follow the manufacturer's primary/booster schedule and the certificate's age-specific requirements; very young pets need the specified two-dose course.

Evidence: [^2][^3][^4]

### 5. Secure the applicable quarantine arrangement

Conditional · Before import; reserve after a qualifying titre result when quarantine is needed

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Schedule III pets require at least 30 days at the Animal Quarantine Centre and rabies vaccination on arrival.
- A personal Schedule II pet qualifies for no quarantine only with the specified more-than-6-month same-address ownership history and arrival less than 5 days from the owner's entry. Otherwise seek approved home quarantine of at least 10 days; commercial imports use AQC.
- Schedule I pets do not routinely require quarantine when their conditions are met.

Evidence: [^1][^3][^4]

### 6. License the pet and obtain the import licence

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arrival; import licence valid 90 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply for the dog or cat licence through PALS first.
- Obtain the AVS import licence through the current import service. Published normal processing is 2 working days, but leave time for missing information and quarantine coordination.

Evidence: [^1]

### 7. Complete final treatments and official certification

Required within the applicable pathway · Veterinary certificate within 7 days; parasite treatments 2–7 days before export

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Have the government-approved veterinarian examine the pet and record internal and external parasite treatments.
- Have the government veterinary authority endorse the correct AVS certificate, and carry original vaccination records. For Schedules I/II, prepare the required route/transport declaration.

Evidence: [^2][^3][^4]

### 8. Book inspection and prepare border documents

Required within the applicable pathway · Book inspection at least 5 days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Arrange inspection at CAPQ for air entry or Tuas for land entry, and bring the licence, certificate and supporting records.
- Use a suitable rigid transport crate for required transfers. Check whether a Customs/GST permit is needed for the specific accompanied or relocation arrangement.

Evidence: [^1]

## Departure: exporting from Singapore

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Arrange destination veterinary preparation

Required within the applicable pathway · Before booking; tests and waiting periods are destination-specific

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's current import permit and health requirements and arrange the required veterinary work.
- Have a private veterinarian examine the pet and complete the destination certificate where required.

Evidence: [^5]

### 2. Obtain the AVS export licence

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure; licence valid 90 days

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply through the stated AVS/GoBusiness service and enter the correct animal and journey details.
- Allow the published 2-working-day normal processing period plus time for corrections; express service is a separate option.

Evidence: [^5]

### 3. Obtain government endorsement when required

Conditional · After veterinary certification and within the destination's validity window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply for AVS endorsement if the destination requires it, submitting the application and required original veterinary, vaccine and laboratory records.
- Follow AVS's collection or verifiable-certificate process and carry the destination-required originals. Government endorsement is not asserted as mandatory for every destination.

Evidence: [^5]

## Caveats

- Draft research, not human-approved. Minimum quarantine can be extended.
- A pet's entry close to its owner is not sufficient alone for the Schedule II quarantine exemption.
- Carrier transport acceptance and customs tax treatment require separate confirmation.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- No issued import licence, home-quarantine approval or pet-specific residence evidence was inspected.
- The complete breed/hybrid list and housing occupancy limits are not reproduced.
- Current web licence guidance was preferred to older service-platform wording embedded in veterinary PDFs.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
