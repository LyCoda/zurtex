# Mainland China: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft personal-entry pathway under GACC's one-pet-per-traveller regime. Designated-origin status, identity, certificates and inspection determine whether the ordinary 30-day quarantine requirement can be waived.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### GACC designated jurisdictions in the catalogue

Catalogue jurisdictions: GB, IE, PT, CH, SG, HK, JP, AU, NZ.

Requires accepted originating-jurisdiction evidence, readable chip, official documents and satisfactory inspection. Hawaii and Guam are designated US regions; the rest of US is not. [^2][^1]

### Non-designated-origin titer or quarantine pathway

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, FR, DE, NL, ES, IT, NO, AE, TW, TH, MY, PH.

US excludes qualifying Hawaii and Guam movements. A recognized laboratory report, appropriate vaccination history and inspection are needed for a quarantine exemption. [^2][^1][^3]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Confirm the personal-pet entry pathway and origin status

Required within the applicable pathway · Before booking transport

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- The personal-entry rule permits one dog or cat per person per entry; match the traveller's name and passport details with the animal's certificate.
- Establish the actual originating jurisdiction and movement history before claiming a designated-origin exemption. Cargo, multiple animals, sale or ownership-transfer movements require separate customs review.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 2. Identify the animal with a readable microchip

Required within the applicable pathway · Before preparing the final certificates

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Use an ISO 11784/11785 chip or provide a compatible reader for an accepted non-standard chip.
- Record the chip number and required implantation details on the official certificate and ensure the animal, vaccination and laboratory records can be linked consistently.

Evidence: [^2]

### 3. Document accepted rabies vaccination

Required within the applicable pathway · Vaccination must remain valid on entry; follow the exporting authority's China certificate

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the official rabies vaccination documentation and use the manufacturer's stated validity rather than imposing an unsupported universal interval.
- For the non-designated pathway, prepare the vaccination history needed for the antibody test. The U.S. certificate pathway specifies two lifetime doses and a currently valid dose; obtain confirmation for another origin's exact certificate terms.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 4. Obtain approved antibody evidence for a non-designated origin

Conditional · Sample no earlier than the second rabies vaccination; report valid for 1 year

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- To seek exemption from quarantine for a non-designated origin, use a GACC-recognized laboratory and carry the original report with the animal's microchip identity.
- Official English sources disagree between 'above 0.5' and 'at least 0.5 IU/ml'; have the receiving authority confirm acceptance of an exact 0.5 result instead of automatically marking it eligible.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 5. Obtain the exporting government's quarantine certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Clinical examination within 14 days before arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Arrange the required health examination and official certificate, including chip details, rabies records and applicable laboratory information.
- Carry the original documents or the officially accepted printed electronic certificate for that exporting authority; private veterinary records alone do not replace government certification.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 6. Choose a port that can handle the animal's quarantine outcome

Required within the applicable pathway · Before confirming the flight

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- If the animal will require quarantine, use a port with designated quarantine facilities; using an unsuitable port can lead to return or disposal under GACC rules.
- Book for a possible 30-day quarantine if the exemption criteria are not met. Confirm the port's documentary and cargo arrangements directly rather than assuming an antibody report guarantees release.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

### 7. Declare the pet and complete entry inspection

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival; subsequent dog registration according to local rules

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Declare the animal, present its official health and rabies documents and any antibody report, and make the microchip available for inspection.
- After entry, check the destination city's dog registration, breed and size restrictions; national border admission does not establish that keeping the dog is lawful in every city.

Evidence: [^1][^2][^3]

## Departure: exporting from Mainland China

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Complete the destination's conditions and confirm the local Customs procedure

Required within the applicable pathway · Before booking the export inspection

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain the destination's import permit and certificate requirements and complete any required chip, vaccination, titer and treatment sequence.
- Contact the exporting Customs office about personal versus cargo/commercial handling. The detailed online workflow verified here is Shanghai's and is not a universal national appointment deadline.

Evidence: [^4]

### 2. Apply for the official pet export quarantine service

Required within the applicable pathway · Shanghai planning guidance: apply about 10–14 days before departure, adjusted to the destination's certificate window

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- For Shanghai personal exports, apply through Internet + Customs under the non-commercial pet export quarantine service with owner and pet details, pet photo and vaccination/test records.
- Prepare the original passport or ID and locally required documents, including dog registration where applicable; a representative needs the required authorization.

Evidence: [^4]

### 3. Attend the Customs inspection and obtain the export certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Within the destination's examination and certificate-validity windows

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Bring the animal and original supporting documents to the appointed Customs veterinary inspection.
- Collect the official animal health certificate after approval and check the destination's validity period. Shanghai's suggested appointment and processing periods are planning estimates, not permission to depart without the certificate.

Evidence: [^4]

## Caveats

- Draft pending human approval; GACC's Chinese announcement prevails over an inconsistent English summary.
- The Shanghai FAQ suggests an Australian rabies-vaccination exemption, while the general GACC rule requires rabies documentation; no automatic Australian vaccine waiver is coded.
- A later Shanghai FAQ's port wording appears broader than GACC's quarantine-port rule; confirm the actual port for each exemption case.
- Assistance-dog exemptions require the specified documentary evidence and inspection; they are not inferred from an owner's description.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- The underlying GACC Chinese attachment was unavailable during this check; its official government republication and newer Shanghai/APHIS guidance were used.
- Exact originating-country/residence proof, the 0.5 IU/ml boundary and any Australian vaccination waiver require direct confirmation.
- Export procedures outside Shanghai, commercial shipments, local breed prohibitions and hybrid-cat wildlife controls require separate verification.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
