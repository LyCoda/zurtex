# United Arab Emirates: dog and cat travel research

Research snapshot: 2026-09-11. Status: researched with gaps. Publication: draft. Human approval: none.

[Research index](../README.md) · [Scope, findings and conflict register](../2026-09-11-country-requirements.md)

## Scope and conclusion

Draft follows the current MOCCAE Arabic service and flags older conflicting English guidance. Risk classification does not establish unrestricted origin eligibility.

This dossier records both entry requirements and departure procedures. Its origin groups describe potential pathways, not a finding that an individual pet meets residence, vaccination, ownership or permit conditions. It is not an exhaustive commercial-import, assistance-animal, wildlife or local-keeping-law opinion.

## Origin-dependent pathways

### Potential low-rabies-risk pathway in current Arabic service

Catalogue jurisdictions: AU, GB, IE, JP, HK, SG, NZ, PT, CH, NL, ES.

Spain excludes Ceuta and Melilla. This classification is not confirmation of residence, origin approval or permit eligibility. [^1]

### Not listed as low risk in current Arabic service

Catalogue jurisdictions: US, CA, FR, DE, IT, NO, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH.

Use the high-risk titre pathway provisionally and confirm against the issued permit; older lists differ. [^1]

## Entry: preparation and arrival actions

The order below is the editorial baseline. Select the appropriate branch and arrange early permit, quarantine and appointment tasks alongside veterinary preparation; do not wait until an earlier procedure is finished to start booking a later appointment. The application filters species/countries and stops unapproved direct AU/NZ pathways.

### 1. Check animal and origin eligibility

Planning advice · Before preparations

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Check MOCCAE's prohibited-dog list and hybrid-cat pedigree conditions before booking.
- Confirm origin restrictions separately from rabies risk; the linked approved-country page was inaccessible.

Evidence: [^1]

### 2. Obtain the MOCCAE import permit

Required within the applicable pathway · Before shipment; current service states 90-day validity

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply online and keep the issued permit valid for arrival.
- Personal imports are normally limited to two pets per person annually; returning-resident exceptions apply.

Evidence: [^1][^2]

### 3. Complete identification and required vaccinations

Required within the applicable pathway · Rabies vaccination from 12 weeks of age; maintain validity

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Match the permanent microchip across the official certificate and vaccination passport, including vaccine dates and batch details.
- Dogs: rabies, distemper, parvovirus, infectious hepatitis and leptospirosis; the latter has a specified testing alternative.
- Cats: rabies, panleukopenia, rhinotracheitis and calicivirus.

Evidence: [^2][^1]

### 4. Obtain the high-risk-origin antibody test

Conditional · At least 21 days after first or lapsed rabies vaccination

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below. Origin filter: US, CA, FR, DE, IT, NO, CN, TW, TH, MY, PH.

- Use an exporting-authority-approved laboratory; result must be at least 0.5 IU/ml.
- A current booster can avoid that sampling wait. The stated 365-day report validity has continuing-vaccination and subsequent-booster conditions.

Evidence: [^1]

### 5. Complete treatments and official health certification

Required within the applicable pathway · Parasite treatments within 14 days before shipment

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Record internal and external parasite treatment in the health certificate or passport.
- Obtain the exporting veterinary authority's certified health certificate with the required examination and health declarations.

Evidence: [^2][^1]

### 6. Meet age and transit controls

Required within the applicable pathway · At shipment

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Minimum import age: 12 weeks for low-risk origins, 15 weeks for high-risk origins.
- Use IATA-compliant transport; prevent contact with other animals during transit.

Evidence: [^1]

### 7. Present the pet for border release

Required within the applicable pathway · On arrival

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Submit the release application and present permit, certified health certificate, vaccination record and required titre evidence.
- Supply customs/air-waybill details and make the pet available for inspection before release.

Evidence: [^1]

### 8. Use returning-resident evidence when applicable

Conditional · Before departure from UAE and again before return

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Retain the MOCCAE export health certificate and microchip record, and maintain rabies protection without lapse.
- Obtain a return import permit and exporting-country official health certificate; a vaccination lapse triggers first-import treatment.

Evidence: [^2][^1]

## Departure: exporting from United Arab Emirates

These actions run alongside the destination's entry preparation. A destination certificate, import permit, airline reservation and export endorsement are different documents or decisions.

### 1. Prepare the destination's veterinary requirements

Required within the applicable pathway · Before arranging departure

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Obtain destination import conditions and arrange their vaccination, testing and treatment sequence.
- Collect veterinary records and any additional destination-specific declarations for the MOCCAE application.

Evidence: [^4]

### 2. Obtain the MOCCAE export or re-export health certificate

Required within the applicable pathway · Before departure; MOCCAE certificate valid 30 days, subject to shorter destination limits

Species: dogs and cats, subject to the qualifications below.

- Apply and pay online, then present the animal for the required examination and any laboratory testing.
- Obtain the official certificate and carry the destination's required documents. Keep this UAE certificate if planning a return.

Evidence: [^4][^2]

## Caveats

- Draft, not human-approved. Current Arabic service was readable; English and linked-document access was incomplete.
- No routine quarantine duration is promised: release depends on inspection and compliance.
- Official low-risk lists have changed; do not classify all EU countries together.

## Conflicts and remaining verification gaps

- Current MOCCAE says 90-day import-permit validity; USDA's page updated 30 July 2025 still says 30 days. Confirm the issued permit.
- The service's approved-country link failed and its downloadable form was rejected; outbreak suspensions and origin-specific restrictions are unresolved.
- The current Arabic titre-validity wording calls for retesting after a subsequent booster; obtain written clarification before relying on an older report.
- The service's minimum-age wording and older primary-vaccination waiting guidance need reconciliation for very young animals.
- Full prohibited-breed and hybrid-lineage determinations, entry-point acceptance and destination-specific certificate windows require individual review.

These gaps are not waived by the research date. The relevant authority, certificate or issued permit must resolve them before owner approval or individual travel advice.
