# Pet-travel requirements across the Zurtex country catalogue

Research cut-off: 11 September 2026. Edition: country-library draft 2. Status: editorial research with unresolved gaps; no owner approval or public release.

## Executive findings

The catalogue contains 23 jurisdictions, allowing 22 destinations for each starting point. The new research record includes 184 entry actions, 57 departure actions and 129 distinct official-source URLs. Each jurisdiction has a permanent dossier covering both import and export. [Open the full country index](README.md).

A country-pair checklist is not the same as an individual travel decision. Residence history, previous movements, age, ownership, vaccination sequence, document validity, permitted entry points and issued permits can alter the result. For example, the US dog pathway depends on the previous six months of travel, and Japan's designated-region pathway depends on residence as well as departure. [^1][^2]

The product now presents actionable, ordered entry instructions, followed by a separate origin-export section. It uses the reference's reading pattern—status, imperative title, timing, practical bullets and adjacent evidence—while writing original Zurtex copy. The user-supplied PetCleared example is a structure reference, not regulatory authority. Previously completed competitor research is retained in [the earlier flow report](2026-09-11-petcleared-flows-and-ready-pack.md).

This work materially expands the documented baseline. It does **not** establish that every possible requirement, local rule, airline condition, exception or one of the 506 ordered corridors has been individually verified. Significant gaps remain and are listed below and in each dossier. The public-release gate and disabled commercial features remain unchanged.

## Scope

The research covers personally owned dogs and cats, ordinary personal travel and relocation, selected jurisdiction pairings, and the corresponding departure certification processes. It includes identification, rabies, laboratory testing, permits, quarantine, additional vaccines/treatments, certification, arrival procedures, origin classification and known special conditions where evidence was accessible.

Jurisdiction boundaries matter:

- GB means Great Britain, excluding Northern Ireland.
- IE means the Republic of Ireland.
- MY means Peninsular Malaysia; Sabah and Sarawak need their own pathways.
- CN means mainland China; Hong Kong and Taiwan have separate dossiers.
- US country selection is not sufficient to determine Hawaii, Guam or other territorial requirements.
- Mainland/regional scope and excluded territories are recorded in the European dossiers.

The following are not exhaustively covered: sale/adoption/ownership-transfer classification; large groups; breeding and commercial consignments; assistance-animal concessions; CITES/hybrid decisions; every breed or municipal keeping rule; all outbound ports; all connections; carrier-specific acceptance; current quarantine capacity; or a pet's actual medical records. Those are explicit follow-up dimensions, not assumed exemptions.

## Evidence and research method

Destination authorities are the primary source for entry rules. Origin authorities establish who issues or endorses export documents. Current official certificates and legal instruments are preferred to undated summaries when they conflict. A later publication date alone is not treated as proof of a later effective rule. Some official pages were inaccessible; indexed official text or an accessible official counterpart was used only within its stated scope, and the access limitation was retained.

Each source record preserves its title, authority, exact URL, accessed date, publication/update date when recorded, and the claims it supports. Each action references source IDs. The country dossiers provide numbered footnotes and source lists. Search-result text is not represented as inspection of an inaccessible underlying form. None of the research records claims human approval.

The previous source ledger and prior flow report remain intact. The dated JSON under `lib/country-research/` is the structured input to the local preview; the dossiers are the readable research snapshot. The [coverage manifest](../data/country-research-coverage-2026-09-11.json) records the distinction between country research, composition coverage and approval.

## Decision-changing findings

### European Union: use the actual document and origin pathway

The seven EU destinations in this catalogue share the current EU baseline, but national entry procedures still differ. Listed and unlisted non-EU origins must not be mixed. In this catalogue, mainland China, Thailand and the Philippines are unlisted for the reviewed EU non-commercial pathway; an ordinary direct GB origin is listed. [^3]

Identification must precede the rabies vaccination used for travel. Primary rabies vaccination starts at a minimum age of 12 weeks and requires the qualifying waiting period; continuously valid boosters are different. For the applicable unlisted pathway, the current Commission instructions require an approved result of at least 0.5 IU/ml and sampling at least 90 days before certificate issue, not merely a flight chosen three months later. [^4]

EU/passport-equivalent departures, qualifying returning pets and onward travel with a valid certificate need document alternatives. The interface therefore does not infer that departure from an EU country always requires a newly issued passport, or that every third-country departure needs a new AHC regardless of return history. GB-resident travellers have a specific AHC rule. [^5][^6]

For the directly illustrated GB-issued certificate, the ten-day planning window includes its issue day. An intended arrival of 25 September 2026 therefore displays 16–25 September as the possible issue window, subject to every other condition. It does not assert that the appointment is booked or that the pet is ready. The new certificate model transition also needs date-specific checking. [^7][^8]

Ireland's dog-specific tapeworm requirement and applicable direct-origin exemptions must not be copied into cat instructions. Portugal's non-EU arrival process requires advance notice to the designated entry point, including its operating hours. These examples show why one generic EU arrival card is insufficient. [^5][^9]

### United States and Canada: species, history and purpose come first

US dog requirements branch on six-month travel history and whether rabies vaccination was issued in the US or abroad. Low-risk departure alone does not establish the simpler pathway. Cats have a distinct federal pathway and must not inherit dog-only CDC tasks. Hawaii and Guam require separate local preparation; the interface now keeps regional actions scoped and flags an unspecified arrival region. [^10][^11][^12][^13][^14][^15]

Canada requires the actual CFIA scenario: species, age, purpose and accompaniment can change the evidence. Commercial dog definitions and the high-risk-country prohibition are separate from ordinary personal-pet conditions; a vaccination statement alone cannot establish the correct pathway. [^16][^17]

### Australia and New Zealand: approved origins are an entry gate

Australia's approved-country pathways and New Zealand's categorised-country pathways impose preparation and history conditions. Unlisted direct departures cannot be made eligible by displaying the ordinary checklist. The local composer replaces standard direct-entry instructions with an approved-exporting-country action for the relevant unsupported origins. A short connection does not establish the required residence. [^18][^19]

Australia's ordinary Group 3 quarantine minimum and a shorter qualifying pathway depend on preparation and identity evidence; a universal ten-day promise would be wrong. New Zealand is in a transition between standards, and the final standard includes identity exceptions that are more specific than a general six-month summary. Complete certificate clauses and outstanding dog-panel details remain pre-publication work. [^20][^21]

### East and Southeast Asia: retain distinct permit and testing sequences

Japan's non-designated pathway, Hong Kong's origin groups, Taiwan's ordinary quarantine versus waiver pathways and Singapore's schedules are different systems. None should inherit another authority's country list or waiting-period anchor. [^22][^23][^24][^25]

Singapore's simpler branch depends on qualifying history. The result retains conditional rabies/testing alternatives when that history has not been established, even when the flight leaves Great Britain, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand. Similar conditional safeguards apply to lower-risk Australia and New Zealand branches. [^26][^27]

Thailand's vaccination instructions conflict on whether the primary waiting period precedes departure or permit application. The Philippines' current page contains minimum-age wording and younger-animal exceptions that need authority reconciliation. Malaysia's revised scheduled-country notice conflicts with older index/protocol wording. These are documented holds, not silently selected convenient interpretations. [^28][^29][^30][^31]

### Departure requirements are independently useful

Entry permission does not replace the origin's export process. The research records, among others, USDA endorsement, CFIA endorsement before departure, AQS export inspection, AFCD certification, AVS export licensing, DVS/MAQIS documents, APHIA certification, BAI documentation, MOCCAE certification and European local-authority procedures. [^32][^33][^34][^35][^36][^37][^38][^39][^40]

The product shows these as a parallel departure section. It does not number an export certificate after the destination's arrival check, and does not invent one certificate validity period for all destinations.

## Product implementation and verification boundaries

The local result now has visible numbered actions, practical bullets, official references, separate entry/departure reading counts and an explanatory country-pathway disclosure. A read tick never means compliant. Supported calendar notes retain their conditions; hour-based treatment windows, calendar-month waits, quarantine completion and earliest feasibility are not computed from an arrival date alone. “Urgent” or “overdue” is not inferred from missing records.

The Ready Pack preview consumes the same actions, preserves blockers and conditionality, separates departure work, and includes the relevant citations in Preparation and print. It remains an in-memory working preview: no purchased pack, reviewed PDF, payment, upload, account, notification or promised reviewer action has occurred.

Tests exercise all 506 ordered pairs for both species (1,012 combinations), source-reference resolution, publication gating, direct-only treatment exemptions, lower-risk history alternatives, US-cat separation, unsupported direct origin stops and date boundaries. These tests establish implementation behaviour, not the correctness of every regulatory assertion.

Source-level independent review was used for the changed interface. Its four material fixes—blocker continuity, separate package sequences and evidence, scoped counts, and updated design documentation—were resolved. No fresh rendered browser or print-pagination approval is claimed for this iteration.
